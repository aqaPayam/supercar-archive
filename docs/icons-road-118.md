## Admission and identity

The fifth-generation GR Supra revived a major Japanese sports-car name as the first global GR road model. Car and Driver’s original 2020 10Best assessment supports its responsive handling and driver communication while acknowledging restricted visibility and divisive styling. This modern two-seat coupe represents the A90 road generation once across engine outputs, transmissions, editions and years.

Complete identities were checked for Supra, A90/A91, J29 and DB42 aliases.
Only A60, A70 and A80 Supras were present; PA90 belongs to Isuzu 117. The
existing BMW G29 Z4 is a separately named roadster programme. Shared BMW-derived
running gear is acknowledged and does not turn the Toyota coupe into a Z4
alias. Count the A90 road generation once across engines, power changes,
manual/automatic choices, later editions and model years. Neither FT-1 concepts
nor GT4 competition cars supply this road specification.
