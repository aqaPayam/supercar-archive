## Plymouth Valiant / Duster

The Duster 340 established Plymouth’s lightweight fastback compact-muscle formula, combining the 340 V8 with practical rear seating and a low-cost performance package. Its distinct Valiant-derived body and historical sporting role justify one original Duster family entry. The contemporary test praised its engine and transmission but reported severe understeer, heavy steering and highway hunting; significance is not a claim of excellent all-round handling.

## Dodge Coronet

The Coronet Super Bee was Dodge’s dedicated no-frills Scat Pack muscle coupe, pairing the 383 Magnum with a standard four-speed, heavy-duty brakes and Rallye suspension. That purpose-built road-performance identity and established place in the muscle-car era justify one Coronet family entry. It is a distinct Coronet body/model programme from the Charger and Plymouth Road Runner, not another engine option or paint scheme for those records.

## Evidence actually inspected

All four PDF pages of the March 1970 Car Life Duster 340 test, printed pages
46–50, were rendered and visually read. The selected car has a 340 V8,
three-speed TorqueFlite, 3.23 axle and manual steering. The test praises the
engine/transmission but reports severe understeer, abrupt power oversteer,
heavy steering and highway hunting. Suggested tuning and a rear anti-roll
bar are not represented as factory equipment. Its text and equipment panel
differ on disc-brake standard/optional wording, so brakes are described only
as fitted. No speculative power, measured performance, production count or
unconfirmed differential is imported. The Carter model designation is not
resolved from the scan and is omitted. A Dodge heritage URL redirected to a
current news hub; that hub was not used as evidence for the original car.

The 12-page Dodge factory catalogue was downloaded. A contact sheet located
the Coronet spread, PDF index 3 (printed pages 6–7), and Scat Pack spread,
PDF index 6 (printed pages 12–13); both were visually read at full size.
The selected Super Bee 383 Magnum row specifies 335 hp at 5,200 rpm and
425 lb-ft at 3,400 rpm, distinct from the Dart 383 row. Standard four-speed,
Rallye suspension and heavy-duty 11-inch drums are retained. Optional disc
brakes, automatic, Ramcharger induction, 440/Hemi/A12 hardware, an axle ratio
and modern net/gross labels are not inferred. The other catalogue pages
were not all read. No extra count is assigned for engine or body options.
