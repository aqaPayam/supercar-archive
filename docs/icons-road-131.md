# Road-icon batch 131: Porsche 904 Carrera GTS street car

Reviewed 2026-10-30 against all 927 existing identities. One absent original
904 production-road family brings the working inventory to 928: 533 curated

This is not a certified qualifying count toward the 1,000-icon goal.

## Admission, road eligibility and identity

The 904 combines a bonded GRP body/chassis, mid-engine design and enduring
endurance-GT significance. The original production street model qualifies
within the confirmed road-car scope. This is not an assumption that any
historic racer belongs: Porsche explicitly describes the 904 as designed for
road and racetrack, and holding museums document original street examples.

The complete inventory and all Porsche/904/Carrera GTS identities were checked.
No original Type 904 family was present. Count all road outputs, equipment,
years and individual chassis once. Works six/eight-cylinder versions, racing
tunes and individual competition chassis are not additional road entries.

The selected reference is **1964 four-cam street car, Audrain Begovich Collection**.
Its museum-listed specification remains an identified holding-museum reference,
not a claim that all street or race 904s had identical figures.

## Selected technical evidence

The full relevant [Audrain collection account and panel](https://www.audrainautomuseum.org/1964-porsche-carrera-gts-904-1)
were read. Eight attributes map directly to it: nominal 2.0 L, four-cam flat-four,
listed 153 hp at 6900 rpm, 125 ft-lb at 5000 rpm, five-speed manual, mid-engine
layout, fibreglass body bonded to chassis and coil springs. The power-rating
standard is unspecified; the hp label is retained without inventing DIN/SAE
or converting to PS. No performance, mass, production total, exact cc, drag
coefficient, gear ratios or broad originality superlatives are imported.

Porsche's [911 GTS press-kit history](https://newsroom.porsche.com/dam/jcr%3A51ff1da3-7997-4859-9bc8-b44c8994d149/PAG-911-GTS-EN.pdf)
was downloaded as a 27-page PDF. History index 25/printed p.26 was individually
rendered and read; an overview paragraph at index 4 was also read as extracted
text. No complete visual-document audit is claimed. The section identifies
F.A. Porsche's original road-and-track design, first GTS, plastic body and
26 November 1963 unveiling. Targa Florio is programme context, not a street
car's individual competition result. Other GTS-model figures are not used.

The [FIA Group GT index](https://historicdb.fia.com/car/porsche-904-gts) was read
in full relevant context and controls the ninth attribute, rear-wheel drive.
The [original form](https://historicdb.fia.com/sites/default/files/car_attachment/1600928401/homologation_form_number_159_group_gt.pdf)
was downloaded; only the initial cover and engine sheet were individually
inspected. Its 180 PS DIN at 7200 rpm tune is not assigned to the Audrain
street car. Other rendered pages were not inspected or used. Exact recognition
day labels differ between cover and registry; no exact-day claim is imported.
Later changes, race tuning and event-entry paperwork supply no selected fields.

Porsche's [museum account](https://newsroom.porsche.com/en/press-kits/Porsche-Museum/Porsche-904-Carrera-GTS.html)
was also read: its table explicitly describes an eight-cylinder 240-hp example,
not this road four-cylinder. It is research context only, not an additional
selected specification source. A generic model heading is insufficient to
merge a works engine into the street record.

## Independent museum context and limitations

The complete relevant [Revs collection account](https://revsinstitute.org/vehicle/1964-porsche-carrera-gts)
and panel were read. GRP/chassis production, endurance history and lasting
design support admission, with body-thickness variation, difficult entry,
driver-error sensitivity and gearbox limitations retained. Its 904 046 and
180 HP at 7000 rpm panel are another museum reference; none of those technical
values are copied into the selected car. Race results of #046 do not imply
every production street car competed.

Revs' [2022 road-car story](https://museum.revsinstitute.org/news/1964-porsche-904-carrera-gts/)
was read in its full relevant text. It explicitly identifies original and current
street legality of #046, while distinguishing removal of road equipment and
engine tuning for racing. Its embedded video was not watched. This is additional
road-scope context, not an extra database source or authority for Audrain's
particular output. Exact mappings are in the [manifest](../data/icons-road-131.json).
