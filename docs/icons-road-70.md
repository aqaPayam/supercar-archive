## Evidence boundaries

The original Nash brochure was downloaded and rendered. Its specification
spread and manufacturer imprint were visually read. The selected reference is
the June 1953 US-market Pinin Farina convertible. It retains native displacement,
power/torque units, manual transmission with automatic overdrive, chassis and
brake details. Automatic overdrive is not an automatic transmission. Its
102-inch wheelbase is distinct from the hardtop's 108-inch wheelbase; no coupe
dimensions are silently transferred. The source does not establish a modern
net/gross standard, so none is invented.

Savoy's collection account establishes the international production process:
Nash drivetrain, Healey rolling chassis and Italian Pininfarina coachwork/final
assembly. United States in the origin field identifies the marque/market
programme and does not mean all manufacture occurred there. A contemporary
July 1952 Motor Sport race report separately records Johnson/Wisdom finishing
third at Le Mans. That lightweight competition car's body, tune and performance
are not the 1953 roadster specification. Museum speed, weight and production
figures and unqualified first-American-sports-car claims are not imported.

Mick Walsh's actual September 2022 road feature documents 1934 TT Replica
BMK 102 and its restoration. It distinguishes original-model identity from
a newly built Meadows engine and an earlier AC six-cylinder conversion. The
fresh engine's modern output, revs and compression are excluded from the 1934
specification. Original-car architecture and driving character are retained,
including cold-brake pull and initial understeer. The marque archive supplies
the Meadows overhead-valve option and aluminium two-seat body in the chain-driven
TT family. Other Blackburne/Gough engines, an apparent displacement typo and
production estimates are not transferred. Chain count, gear count and a
no-differential claim are not guessed from the model's nickname.

The MV1764 owner website failed normal certificate validation and was not used;
no verification was disabled. The Commons text search returned a transient busy
error for the TT, so its public category listing supplied photo metadata instead.
That error was not treated as evidence that no licensed photograph existed.
