## Identity review

The complete catalogue and preceding manifests were checked before import.
C-Class and E-Class AMG generations are absent; existing 190 E, 300 SEL 6.3
and 500 E records do not cover them. One road saloon represents each selected
missing generation. C32, C43, C63 performance packages, separate estates and
extra body styles are not additional entries. W212 and W213 E63 S are distinct
generations, not alternate years of the same car.

The fixed-roof R230 Black Series is distinct from the existing SLR McLaren
and SLS models. No regular SL65, separate SL roadster or SLK Black Series is
added. The original R171 is not confused with R170 SLK32, R172 or the larger
R230 SL: several wrongly returned SL photographs were rejected.

DB7 Vantage belongs to the DB7 family and is not a duplicate of the existing
VH/2022 Vantage records. Original Newport Pagnell Vanquish is distinct from
the existing second-generation Vanquish S. The 2007 DBS is distinct from the
2018 Superleggera and 770 Ultimate. Original-generation S, GT/GTA, Volante,
Jubilee and other additional variants do not multiply the count.

Neither XKR generation nor F-Type previously exists. X100 and X150 have
different steel/aluminium body structures and are represented once each.
No XKR-S, separate convertible, XK180 concept, F-Type SVR or later AWD
variant is imported.

## Evidence and specification boundaries

Factory technical tables were read and visually inspected. Original brochures
are retained as manufacturer evidence even when hosted by a brochure archive;
the host's filename does not override the document itself. Source links,
publishers, document types and individual attribute mappings are public.

- C36: Mercedes-Benz's September 2018 retrospective supplies the original
  3606-cc M104, 206 kW, 385 Nm and four-speed automatic, and documents the
  first joint project. Later five-speed and C43 data are excluded.
- C55: original 2005 US brochure, PDF pages 3–4 and model portrait. The
  362-hp/376-lb-ft V8 column has a five-speed automatic; regular C-Class
  six-speed manual options do not apply. Native US ratings are retained.
- C63: the host filename says US 2009, but the actual English document
  contains international saloon/estate data. Its page-10 technical clipboard
  is dated July 30, 2007 and explicitly gives 6208 cc, 336 kW at 6800 rpm,
  600 Nm and 7G-TRONIC. It is stored as the international launch saloon;
  US 451-hp ratings are not substituted. Manufacturer C-Class history
  independently supports the launch year, dedicated front axle and ESP.
- E55: original 2005 US brochure, PDF page 3 E55 column: 5439 cc,
  supercharger, 469 hp at 6100 rpm, 516 lb-ft and rear-drive five-speed.
  Other E-Class seven-speed and 4MATIC options do not apply.
- W212 E63 S: original 2014 US brochure page 28, S-Model 4MATIC column,
  including the standard-equipment dots for the limited-slip differential.
  Regular 550-hp E63, wagon and optional ceramic brakes are excluded.
- W213 E63 S: the October 2016 US factory release explicitly names MY2018;
  US introduction was in 2017. Its 3982-cc 603-hp/627-lb-ft reference uses
  variable 4MATIC+ and nine-speed MCT. It is not labelled a MY2016 car.
- SL65 Black Series: international original brochure pages 3–4, 8 and 12
  supplies the fixed carbon roof, panels, 5980-cc V12, 493 kW, electronically
  limited 1000 Nm and five-speed automatic. Manufacturer SL history dates
  this R230 derivative to 2008. Driver/luggage-inclusive mass is omitted.
- SLK55: original 2005 US brochure pages 3–4 and roof/engine descriptions.
  It uses 355 hp and 376 lb-ft at 3500 rpm, not the C55's different rating.
  The AMG has a seven-speed automatic and no manual option.
- DB7: Aston Martin's original Vantage profile supplies 5935 cc, 420 bhp,
  400 lb-ft and six-speed manual. Its separate historic-model account
  documents V12 development, Callum styling and more than 4000 Vantages.
  The later 435-bhp GT is excluded.
- Vanquish: the original 460-bhp section is kept separate from the later
  520-bhp S section. ASM/SSM is an electro-hydraulic automated manual,
  not a conventional driver-clutch manual. Film gadgets are not hardware.
- DBS: the manufacturer past-model page supplies the standard manual,
  carbon-ceramic discs and double-wishbone adaptive chassis. The separate
  factory historic-model page supplies 510 bhp. Stunt specifications and
  later Superleggera figures are excluded.
- X100 XKR: Jaguar's complete May 6, 1998 launch pack was retrieved as a
  club-hosted factory facsimile. Scanned PDF pages 2–4, 7 and 9 were rendered
  and visually read. Its table distinguishes DIN 276 kW/525 Nm from EEC
  267 kW/505 Nm. Imported power and torque are explicitly labelled DIN;
  the conventions are not silently combined. This is the original 3996-cc
  five-speed car, not a later 4.2 or the 450-bhp XK180 concept.
- X150 XKR: 2012 US brochure pages 11, 14, 17, 19 and 69. The 510-SAE-hp
  coupe column gives 5000 cc, 461 lb-ft and six-speed automatic; the prose
  names ZF 6HP28. The 550-hp XKR-S and optional Dynamic/appearance packs
  are excluded.
- F-Type R: Jaguar's November 2013 US launch release explicitly lists
  MY2015 pricing and spring 2014 introduction. The original rear-drive
  550-hp coupe has standard 380/376-mm brakes; the post-introduction CCM
  option is not standard. The design award mentioned in the release is
  for the convertible and is not assigned to the R coupe.

Native bhp, US hp, SAE hp and historical DIN ratings retain their conventions.
Unsupported numerical fields and undefined masses remain unfilled.

## Photography and held research

All fourteen selected exterior photographs were visually checked. Wrongly
labelled DB7 convertibles returned by a Vanquish search, R230 SLs returned
for SLK, wrapped/modified XKR images and detail-only pictures were rejected.
The W212 photo uses the ordinary S body rather than the Performance Studio
Special search result. The DBS photograph has accessory wheels. The X150
photo is from the 2012 Chongqing show, not authenticated US equipment.
Gallery captions record these limits and do not authenticate original engines,
transmissions, exact markets or optional equipment. Creators, licences,
source links and unchanged licensed image bytes are retained.

DB9 remains research only: Aston Martin's retrospective combines early VH
history with later 510-bhp engine and brake specifications. A matching dated
reference is needed before import. W205 C63 S is also held: a brochure host's
2016 filename and copyright do not establish its model year. W210 E55 and
C209 CLK63 Black Series are not counted in this batch; the latter would
require the pending same-generation-variant decision.
