## Admission and identity

The Galaxie 427 Sports Hardtop is a defining full-size American muscle-car programme, combining dual-quad FE V8 performance with a purposeful chassis package and the fastback body associated with Ford’s racing effort. An independent first-hand historical evaluation supports its performance significance while distinguishing road cars from lightweight competition specials.

The complete identifier/manufacturer/model/generation/variant inventory was
checked for Galaxie, Starliner, Sunliner and 427 references; no target was
present. Count the 1960–1964 performance-road family once, using the 1963
Galaxie 500 Sports Hardtop with dual-carb 427 and selected four-speed. Roofs,
years, 410/425-bhp engine alternatives and XL trim do not multiply its count.
Lightweight drag specials, NASCAR cars, replicas and later engine changes
are excluded from this road record.
