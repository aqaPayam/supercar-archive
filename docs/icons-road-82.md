## Admission and selected reference

The Matra 530 combined a compact mid-mounted V4 with genuine 2+2 accommodation and a removable two-piece roof. Separate body panels on a welded-sheet structure marked a significant step in Matra’s production-car construction, while independent suspension and four disc brakes supported its sporting role. This distinctive approach to an accessible mid-engine sports car earns one original 530 family entry, separate from the Djet, Bagheera and Murena. A, LX and SX versions do not add separate counts.

## Evidence actually inspected

The ten-page Chrysler France road brochure was downloaded. Narrative page 1,
seats page 3, roof/features page 4, roof-stowage/engine-position page 5,
technical page 6, dimensions/cutaway page 7 and back page 9 were rendered and
visually read. The other pages were not all read. The host labels the scan
1971, but page 9 discusses 1972 results and Le Mans 1972, so that host date is
not accepted as an exact model year. The catalogue year field stays unknown;
it is not derived from the separate representative photograph's file title.

The selected table states 1699 cc, four cylinders in V, 78 ch DIN at 5,000 rpm,
14.4 m.daN at 3,000 rpm, 9:1 compression and a four-speed with all ratios
synchromesh. Its two-piece removable roof can be stowed in the front compartment;
the rear luggage compartment remains distinct from the engine compartment.
The technical page supports independent front/rear suspension, disc brakes and
rear drive. Native power and torque ratings are preserved without substituting
other-source ratings or a modern standard. Neither weight nor road performance
is imported. Brand competition references do not establish that this production
road car won the marque's prototype races or used those race engines.

The actual Matra M530 historical section was read for the original programme
and the change to separate panels on a welded-sheet structure. Its broad
retrospective wording about galvanising is not sufficient to certify the
selected original 530 chassis's corrosion treatment, so that claim is omitted.
The period brochure itself describes an epoxy-resin body. Admission rests on
the combination of construction, mid-engine 2+2 packaging and open-roof sports
layout, rather than the manufacturer's racing prestige alone.
