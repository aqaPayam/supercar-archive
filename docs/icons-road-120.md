## Admission and identity

The W205 C63 carried AMG’s muscular compact sports-saloon tradition into the biturbo-V8 era. The contemporary instrumented road test documents a major handling improvement alongside its strong V8 character, while acknowledging firm ride and imperfect transmission engagement. The C63 S saloon represents the generation once across bodies, outputs, editions and facelift changes.

Complete identities were checked for W205/S205/C205/A205 and 205.086/205.087
aliases, plus every C-Class identity. Only W202 C36, W203 C55 and W204 C63
were present. Count the 205-based C63 road family once across outputs, bodies,
editions and facelift; ordinary AMG Line, C43 and W206 hybrid are excluded.
