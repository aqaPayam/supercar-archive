# Original V8 Audi RS 5 — batch 90

Reviewed 30 October 2026. One absent original performance-road family is admitted.

## Admission and identity

The original RS 5 combined a high-revving naturally aspirated V8 with a significant evolution of quattro: the production debut of Audi’s self-locking crown-gear centre differential and wheel-selective torque vectoring. Its seven-speed dual-clutch drivetrain and dedicated wide-body A5-based coupe establish a substantive performance-engineering case. Count one original V8 RS 5 road family; facelifts, open-top versions, wheel choices and model years do not add entries.

The complete 875-car inventory was searched for RS5, RS 5 and Audi A5 aliases;
all were absent. The full Audi inventory was also read. Existing RS 4 saloon/
estate programmes remain intact: this is the original A5-based RS 5 coupe
with the crown-gear quattro debut, not a new year or trim of those records.
Count the original V8 RS 5 programme once, including its facelift and open-top
derivatives for counting purposes. No later V6, DTM race car or tuned ABT
vehicle is selected. Motorsport naming does not make a road car a DTM racer.

Its admission rests on documented engineering rather than rarity, styling
or a generic power threshold. Audi's original Japanese launch and subsequent
technical history both identify the new crown-gear centre differential. The
high-revving V8 and dual-clutch transmission form the selected road reference.
