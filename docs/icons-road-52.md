## Evidence boundaries

The Fiat manufacturer history distinguishes its initial two-carburettor engine
from subsequent developments. The combined table's speed is qualified by the
narrative as a later-series figure and is omitted from the initial reference.
The factory collection's unique fibreglass shell does not become universal.
The photographed first-style Rapi berlinetta is representative of the family,
not proof of its precise engine tune or current mechanical originality.

The A112's selected early specification comes from the manufacturer's named
collection reference. Later engine and gearbox revisions are not transferred.
The downloaded 1983 owner's manual is not used to substantiate the early car.
Its later-series road photograph is explicitly identified as representative,
including the visible grille, bonnet and bumper differences from the reference.
Competition-liveried images and a photographer-described rebadged Junior were
reviewed and excluded as the main illustration.

Audi Tradition explicitly distinguishes the standard TTS from the track-only
Speed Set and the Jägermeister racing car. The rounded displacement stays
rounded; no unverified exact capacity is inferred. Competition power, speed,
carburettors and ordinary Prinz specifications are excluded. The museum-labelled
TTS photograph is not proof of its precise year or unchanged road equipment.
