Reviewed 2026-10-30 against all 909 identities. One original Fiero road family
brings the working inventory to 910 records: 515 curated road additions across

## Admission and identity

The Fiero is an important American sports-car design experiment, pairing a mid-mounted engine with a spaceframe and replaceable polymer panels. Period recognition and the original tester’s later assessment establish lasting significance despite early shortcomings. The revised 1988 GT represents the whole programme here; it is not claimed to be an outright performance benchmark.

Complete identities were checked for Fiero and P-body aliases, plus every
Pontiac identity; none was present. Existing Pontiac entries are separate
Firebird and GTO families. Count the 1984–1988 Fiero programme once across
years, engines and Coupe/Formula/GT bodies. Racing engines, Ferrari replicas,
swaps and the unbuilt successor do not supply this road reference.

## Technical evidence

The [factory 1988 catalogue](https://xr793.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/02/1988-Pontiac-Full-Line-Prestige.pdf)
is a 37-page scan, 17,859,912 bytes. Full PDF indexes 11 (printed 22–23),
12 (24–25), 31 (62–63) and 32 (64–65) were visually read. Fiero vital and
standard-equipment regions were enlarged and read. The 37-page contact sheet
was navigation only; other rendered pages were not individually audited.

Twenty-two mapped attributes distinguish the GT V6 and WS6 from Coupe Tech IV
and Y99 equipment. The matrix confirms GT inheritance of Formula engine and
suspension, plus the standard five-speed. Optional three-speed automatic and
leather/lumbar seats are not assumed. The GT-specific 164.9-inch length is
kept separate from other bodies. The all-model mass range is not assigned to
this GT. Missing engine peak speeds and gearbox branding are not invented.

The technical summary and vital-specification box give a 22-mm rear stabilizer,
while the standard-equipment list says 23 mm rear. Bar diameters are deliberately
unassigned; the conflict is retained in the manifest. No outside source is used
to silently overwrite the reviewed factory reference. No earlier suspension,
racing tune, unqualified first-American-mid-engine claim or test performance.

[Car and Driver’s 1984 award](https://www.caranddriver.com/features/a15151412/1984-10best-cars/)
recognizes the debut 2M4 programme, not this GT. The
[original tester’s later interview](https://www.caranddriver.com/features/columns/a29513894/remember-the-fiero/)
adds a balanced retrospective; it is historical context, not 1988 technical data.
The relevant Fiero sections were read. A 1986 GT review was located but its
body was not individually audited. SAE abstract access timed out and supplies
no imported field. Neither source is claimed as read technical evidence.

The [manifest](../data/icons-road-113.json) retains exact mappings and limits.
