## Selection and evidence

### Gordon-Keeble GK1

The Gordon-Keeble combined a Chevrolet V8 with a tubular spaceframe, de Dion rear suspension and four-wheel disc brakes in a practical four-seat GT. This ambitious Anglo-American performance design has a documented place in specialist British sports-car history. The production car earns one family entry for its engineering and sporting purpose, rather than rarity alone.

### Cunningham C3

The C3 brought Cunningham’s American V8 sports-car project to road customers with Italian Vignale coachwork. Its production-road programme supported recognition as a manufacturer for the marque’s Le Mans effort. It belongs as an early American high-performance GT with a direct connection to a significant sports-racing programme; the road coupe itself is not credited with the racing cars’ results.
