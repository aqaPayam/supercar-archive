## Duplicate review

The complete 507-record inventory was checked. It contained no 106, RCZ, SM,
or Alpine A110, including alternative manufacturer/family naming. The existing
Peugeot 205 Turbo 16 is a different model and architecture. Renault's 5 Turbo,
Clio Williams and Clio V6 do not represent these families. One road reference
per family is selected; no paint, reference-year, later output or equipment
edition is counted separately. The modern A110 is identified explicitly, so a
future original rear-engined Berlinette cannot be mistaken for this generation.

## Evidence boundaries

- **106:** Peugeot Italia's February 2015 technical retrospective describes
  the original 1294-cc TU2J2 road Rallye introduced in November 1993. Its
  native 98-CV notation is preserved. Later 1.6 engines and competition
  1382-cc figures are excluded. Apparent errors in rear track and fully laden
  mass are omitted. Grassroots competition history explains admission without
  transferring racing specifications to the road car.
- **RCZ R:** Peugeot's December 2013 German launch release describes the
  production car arriving in early 2014, with 199 kW, 330 Nm, twin-scroll
  turbocharging, Torsen differential and four-piston brakes. The earlier
  260-PS concept is excluded. Exact displacement and gearbox are not inferred
  from unrelated RCZ references. Its engineering package supports admission;
  a claim to be the most powerful Peugeot alone would not suffice.
- **SM:** Citroën's May 2015 retrospective explicitly gives the original
  2670-cc six-cylinder, 170 DIN PS at 5500 rpm, front drive and advanced
  chassis/brakes. Native DIN PS is retained. Later injection/3.0 engines and
  rally modifications are excluded. Gearbox and undefined mass remain unfilled.
- **A110:** Alpine's original March 7, 2017 press kit is read through a
  Renault Owners Club facsimile. Pages 2–4 explain the engine, aluminium
  construction and chassis; the page 8 technical table was rendered and
  visually inspected. Directly stated 185 kW / 252 PS and 320 Nm are used.
  The narrative's 1080-kg mass differs from the table's 1103-kg DIN figure,
  so neither becomes a catalogue mass. Later 300-PS, R, GT4 and electric
  specifications are excluded. Launch timing is historical, not a claim
  about current production availability.

All four selected photographs were visually checked. The 106 image is a
1994 Series 1 1.3; the SM is a European enclosed-headlamp exterior without
authenticated build year or engine. The A110 photo identifies a 2018 UK
Première Edition, explicitly distinguished from the continental 2017 launch
reference. The RCZ image shows a production R at Geneva 2014; earlier concept
photos were rejected. Captions preserve these qualifications. Licensed source
bytes, creators, licences and attribution URLs are retained unchanged.

Other French candidates remain outside this import until their precise road
specifications and photographs are resolved. Research does not count toward
the catalogue total.
