Reviewed 2026-10-30 against all 921 existing identities. One absent B110
sporting-road family brings the working inventory to 922: 527 curated road

This is not a certified qualifying count toward the 1,000-icon goal.

## Admission and identity

The B110 Sunny GX is an established foundation of Japanese small-capacity
touring-car racing. Nissan's [complete Heritage Collection account](https://www.nissan-global.com/EN/HERITAGE_COLLECTION/datsun_sunny_1200.html)
explicitly identifies the August 1972 GX-5 road coupe as a race base.
Its close-ratio five-speed with direct fifth is documented separately from
the tuned TS racer. The original road leaflet supports the sporting package;
ordinary Sunny popularity, age or rarity alone does not justify admission.

All identities were checked for Sunny, B110/KB110, Datsun 1200, 1200GX and
GX-5. Token boundaries avoid false matches to Bugatti EB110. There is no
existing Sunny road family. GX/GX-5, sedan/coupe, years and regional Datsun
names count once. Later B210/B310, B120 pickups and dedicated TS racers are
not extra versions of this selected record.

## Original road evidence

Two original Nissan GX-5 leaflet faces preserved by Datsun 1200 Technical
were downloaded and individually inspected:

- [Mechanism face](https://datsun1200.tougetechnologies.com/index.php?title=File:27776.jpg):
  prominent 83 PS / 6400 rpm, torque paragraph 10.0 kg-m / 4400 rpm,
  close-ratio manual diagram and front-disc discussion.
- [Specification face](https://datsun1200.tougetechnologies.com/index.php?title=File:27777.jpg):
  common A12, water-cooled inline-four OHV, 1171 cc and variable-venturi
  twin-carburettor rows; coupe/sedan columns and parenthesized alternatives.

The actual Nissan facsimiles, rather than the community index prose, supply
seven attributes. Two transmission attributes map to Nissan's own narrative:
five-speed floor-shift manual and direct fifth at 1.000. Quoted output uses
the prominent reference without inventing gross/net or fuel-grade certification
from the alternative small-print figures. No top speed, measured acceleration,
uncertain ratios, mass, dimensions or general equipment is imported.

Nissan's Heritage Collection table is explicitly headed Competition and notes
a later 1975-specification engine. That 1298 cc / 145 PS engine, 638 kg mass,
racing carburettors and tyres do not supply road attributes. The narrative's
tuned racing output likewise remains competition history. FIA form 5356 was
downloaded and its variant page at PDF index 14 inspected for navigation;
its unnamed variant is not assumed to certify the selected GX-5 equipment.
No FIA technical field is copied into this manifest.

The [manifest](../data/icons-road-125.json) retains exact source mappings and limits.
