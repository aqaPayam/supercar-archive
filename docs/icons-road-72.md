## Admission and selected reference

The C-V8 paired substantial Chrysler V8 performance with a British four-seat GT body. A contemporary long-distance road test documents its exceptional acceleration and relaxed fast touring, while recording heavy steering and demanding brakes. Its distinct centre-tube chassis and sporting-road identity justify one original family entry, separate from the later FF and Jensen-Healey.
