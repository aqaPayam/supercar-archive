# GR86: second-generation 86/BRZ road programme — batch 89

Reviewed 30 October 2026. One absent sports-coupe generation is admitted.

## Admission and identity

The GR86 advances the lightweight, front-engine/rear-drive 86 sports-coupe concept with a larger 2.4-litre boxer, a newly developed body and increased rigidity while retaining a six-speed manual and driver-focused chassis. It represents the second-generation Toyota/Subaru 86/BRZ road programme, distinct from the original 2.0-litre GT86 already preserved. Count this shared generation once; Subaru badges, grades, transmissions, anniversaries and model years do not add separate entries.

The complete 874-car inventory was checked for GR86, GR 86, ZN8, ZD8 and
BRZ identities; all were absent. The existing TOY-GT86-UK2012 is the original
ZN6 2.0-litre generation and remains intact. Toyota's new production release
identifies the joint Subaru development, newly developed body and larger
2.4-litre engine. This entry covers the whole second-generation shared
programme once. Another BRZ badge, trim, transmission, anniversary, model
year or competition derivative cannot increase this family's count.

The engineering case is the substantial evolution of a purpose-built,
front-engine/rear-drive sports coupe retaining a manual gearbox and driver
focus. Independent recognition is separately recorded: the GR86 and BRZ
jointly received Car and Driver's 2023 10Best award. That editorial supplies
no Japanese technical specifications.
