## Admission and identity

The TE27 established the original Levin/Trueno sporting lineage by putting
the Celica's twin-cam engine in a smaller road coupe. Toyota's [early Corolla
motorsport history](https://global.toyota/en/corolla50th/history/ms/) explicitly
connects this 1972 production family with its early WRC programme. This is
substantial sporting heritage beyond ordinary Corolla popularity or rarity.

All current identities were checked for TE27 and Toyota Corolla/Sprinter/E20.
Only the later AE86 Levin and Trueno appear. The original TE27 is absent.
Toyota explicitly links the sister Levin and Trueno to the same 20-series
2T-G road programme. Sister badge, years, grades and facelift count once here.
The ordinary TE25/OHV coupe, US SR5, race specification and later generations
are not substituted for the selected road car.

## Road reference and competition boundary

The [Toyota UK model history](https://mag.toyota.co.uk/toyota-corolla-generations-1970-74/)
was read in its complete relevant article body. Its original 1972 2T-G Levin
reference supplies seven attributes: engine designation, DOHC layout,
displacement, Solex carburetion, quoted power/torque and kerb mass.
Output remains the source's quoted PS with unspecified test basis, not an
asserted net/SAE figure or a calculated conversion. General sporting-model
gearbox or Corolla equipment statements are not exact Levin equipment proof;
no transmission, brakes, tyres, dimensions or compression ratio is imported.

The complete relevant early-Corolla section of Toyota's global motorsport
history was read. Its production eight-valve statement supplies one separate
technical attribute. Its later sixteen-valve 151E competition description
does not become this road engine. The UK article compresses the rally story,
so no claim that the standard 115-PS road engine won the 1975 event is made.
The global account separately identifies another early winning TE25/2T-B
car; that claim supplies no road fields or admission fact here. Rally results
explain the lineage, not certification of the photographed or stock road car.

The Toyota 75-year lineage link reached the ordinary 1970 Corolla coupe
table (TE25/KE25, not TE27). It was read and deliberately supplies no selected
TE27 attributes. Its generic output footnote is not evidence for relabelling
the TE27 engine's rating. The museum asset page is not a technical table.
Exact source mappings and limits are in the [manifest](../data/icons-road-124.json).
