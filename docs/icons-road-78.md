## Mercury Cougar

The Cougar Eliminator is Mercury’s distinctive contribution to the pony-car performance era. The Boss 302 road configuration combined a high-output small-block V8 with a required four-speed in the Cougar’s own hardtop body and longer-wheelbase layout. This mechanical identity and established performance-package history justify one original Cougar family entry; spoilers, colours and alternative engines do not create additional cars.
