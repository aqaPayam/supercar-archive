## Admission and identity

The Alfonso XIII belongs for its role in the development of the production
sports car from competition engineering. The road-family history is supported
by the [Revs two-seater account](https://revsinstitute.org/vehicle/1912-hispano-suiza-15t-alfonso-xiii)
and the named sporting model in the
[manufacturer timeline](https://www.hispanosuizacars.com/our-history/).
An exclusive first-sports-car assertion is unnecessary and is not imported.
Royal association, price and rarity alone are not admission reasons.

A fresh complete 936-record identity inventory was checked for Hispano-Suiza,
Alfonso/Alphonso XIII, 15T, 15-45 and 15/45 aliases. No existing family was
found. The original road family is counted once across years, chassis,
coachwork and road racing appearances. The Revs short and long chassis are
different reference cars within this family, not two archive additions.
Dedicated competition machines, replicas and later Hispano programmes do
not become additional records in this batch.

Preservation and original-paint claims remain attributed custodian accounts,

only. Its displacement rounding, output/rpm, wheelbase, weight and seating
