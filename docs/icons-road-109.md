## Admission and identity

The Adventurer is a significant early American performance programme: its 1957 factory-standard dual-carb Hemi advertised 345 brake horsepower from a nominal 345 cubic inches. This engineering benchmark and the programme’s continuing recognition in specialist museum collections justify its place beyond rarity, luxury trim or price.

Complete identities were checked for DeSoto, De Soto, Adventurer, Fireflite,
Firedome and Firesweep aliases; none was present. Count the original 1956–1960
Adventurer road programme once across years, engines and bodies, reviewing
associated Fireflite identities together. Ghia concepts and later wedge or
fuel-injected specifications are not this selected 1957 Hemi hardtop.

## Technical evidence

The [original factory AMA sheets](https://over-drive-magazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/1957-DeSotoAMA-Specs-1-24.pdf)
contain 25 scanned pages, 5,224,404 bytes despite the filename ending 1-24.
Full PDF indexes 0, 1, 5, 12, 14 and 18 were visually read, with the revised
15 December 1956 Adventurer column distinguished from Fireflite and Firesweep.
Other rendered pages were not individually viewed.

Twenty mapped attributes preserve nominal 345-cu-in displacement, advertised
345 bhp at 5200 rpm, 355 lb-ft at 3600 rpm, dual Carter carburettors, standard
TorqueFlite and specific wheels/brakes. The factory test-accessory convention
is recorded; there is no modern net conversion. Sheet 19 states Adventurer
body data refer to a special club coupe, two-door hardtop. Default sedan
measurements and missing shipping mass do not become hardtop curb weight.
No measured displacement, racing camshaft or universal priority claim.

The [Newport Car Museum collection profile](https://newportcarmuseum.org/3803-2/)
provides independent recognition for the programme, featuring its convertible
as Car of the Month in October 2022. It is not the technical authority for the
selected hardtop: the museum’s PowerFlight listing conflicts with the factory
TorqueFlite sheet. Its full-race-cam phrase, unqualified first-production-car
claim, times, mass, price and production totals are not imported. Factory
standard equipment establishes the engineering benchmark without claiming a
world first. The second public fact explicitly identifies the museum’s body.

The Over-Drive secondary fact sheet located documents only. A National DeSoto
Club history-page request returned 429 and was not retried. A different club
forum page was readable, but its pasted retrospective contains corrections;
it supplies no technical fields or first-hand road-test claim here. Search-only
Hemmings and collection leads were not treated as read evidence.
