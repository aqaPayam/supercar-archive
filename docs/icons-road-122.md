## Admission and identity

The original Mazdaspeed3 combines direct-injection turbo power, manual control
and limited-slip traction. The contemporary [2007 Car and Driver 10Best
assessment](https://www.caranddriver.com/features/a15144842/2007-10best-cars/)
supports its engaging performance-hatchback character and acknowledges torque
steer. This assessment, rather than factory superlatives or power alone,
supports admission. No optional dealer tuning gain or test acceleration is imported.

The full inventory has no Mazda3, Mazda 3, Mazdaspeed, Axela or MPS alias.
All 16 existing Mazda identities were checked. The original manufacturer's
release explicitly identifies Mazda3 MPS in Europe and Mazdaspeed Axela in Japan
as regional names for Mazdaspeed3. They are grouped into this single original
BK entry, including years and grades. Later BL, ordinary Mazda3 and modern
Mazda3 Turbo are not additional versions of this selected reference.
