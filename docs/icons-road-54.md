## Technical and historical evidence boundaries

The M3 announcement is dated September 2020, but introduction is March 2021.
The original manual saloon retains its own power and transmission rather than
taking Competition, M xDrive, Touring, CS or facelift equipment. Optional ceramic
brakes and buckets are not universal. The combined release's maximum torque
is not assigned to the base manual. Andrew Frankel's actual contemporary test
praises the new engine but criticises the UK Competition car's weight and
automatic response. It is acknowledged as a mixed assessment, not presented
as an unqualified endorsement or the manual car's specification source.

The FL5 uses the September 2022 Japan launch reference. Native kW/Nm and the
published model table supply its selected road specification. The first-hand
Honda engineering history explains the FK8-to-FL5 development and Type R
continuity. No US horsepower, European tune, unprinted rpm, special record-car
configuration, comparable-course lap claim or later package is inferred. A
separate European press link returned an access error and is not relied upon.

Nissan explicitly designates the 2023 Z as seventh generation. This is not a
claim that every chassis component is clean-sheet. The selected Performance
manual includes its own differential and brakes, not universal Sport equipment.
The USA torque band is retained rather than mixed with another market's page.
The V6/24-valve description establishes four valves per cylinder. Proto Spec
and Nismo do not create extra entries.

Nissan's own HS130 heritage record supplies the 1982 Japanese two-seater's
engine and chassis, and dates the S130 full-model change to 1978. The T suffix
here denotes T-top, not a turbo. The first-T-top statement is limited to Japan.
Don Sherman's actual April 1980 US test describes a comfortable GT with capable
cornering and braking but manual drivability shortcomings. It supports that
balanced historical character; none of its US technical values is transferred
to the later Japan reference. No unlisted transmission is guessed.
