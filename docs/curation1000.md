# Curating the archive toward 1,000 cars

User direction recorded 2026-10-28. Baseline: 395 catalogue records.
The goal is 1,000 qualifying records, not simply 605 more database rows.

## Confirmed scope

Performance icons: supercars, sports cars, JDM legends, rally icons and
exceptional muscle cars. Ordinary cars do not qualify merely because they
are old, rare, expensive, powerful or available in a manufacturer archive.

Every proposed addition must have a concise, evidence-backed reason to belong:
landmark engineering, substantial motorsport heritage, lasting cultural
significance or a widely established place in performance-car history.
Manufacturer marketing superlatives alone are insufficient.

## Questions still awaiting the user's answers

- Count significantly different factory variants separately, or group all
  variants within one model generation?
- Include dedicated racing cars and historically significant working
  prototypes, or restrict additions to production road cars/track derivatives?

Candidate research can proceed while these are pending. Original production
road models absent from the archive fit either counting option; prioritize
these while leaving additional variants, dedicated racers and prototypes pending.
Research candidates never count as catalogue additions.

## Admission workflow

1. Explain why the car belongs before collecting specifications or images.
2. Compare the complete existing inventory, including alternative names,
   generations and road/competition identities. An unfamiliar spelling or
   changed reference year is not a new car.
3. Resolve exact factory specification, year/market, body, engine and road-use
   status. Racing achievements may explain a road homologation car's relevance,
   but race-car specifications must not be copied into the road record.
4. Collect reliable technical evidence and a licensed representative photograph
   matching the intended model/body. Preserve attribution and qualifications.
5. Import additively, check duplicates, preserve existing records, and verify
   startup and the public export. Keep a dated record of admission reasons.

The research queue is not an approved import manifest. Missing specifications,
photos or identity decisions keep a candidate out of the catalogue.

## Baseline audit

The baseline contains many modern supercar derivatives, while major gaps remain
in European rally homologation cars and historic sporting saloons. Prioritize
these gaps before expanding already well-covered modern families.

An existing Hyundai Verna Iran 1.5 manual record is outside the newly confirmed
performance-icon scope. Preserve it for now and flag it for a separate review;
do not use its presence as precedent for adding ordinary cars. Existing records
are not automatically certified as qualifying under the new policy. The final
1,000-car audit must account for eligibility as well as raw database count.
