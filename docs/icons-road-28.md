## Identity and selection

The complete inventory was searched across identifiers, manufacturer, family,
generation and variant fields for Alpine/A310/A610/GTA, 306/S16/GTI6/GTI-6 and
Venturi/400 aliases. Only the classic and modern A110 families were already
present. None of the three selected families was present. A310 and A610 are
separate road model families, not extra years of the A110. The A610 is explicitly
GTA-derived: no second GTA is counted while the variant boundary is unresolved.
No four-cylinder A310, Pack GT, Albertville/Magny-Cours, different transmission,
cosmetic edition, lighter Rallye, early five-speed S16 or dedicated racer adds a
record. No ordinary 306 is admitted.

Venturi 400 GT remains on research hold. Original brochure catalogue entries and
secondary articles were located, but a readable original road technical document
was not established in this review. Trophy/GTR racers and road conversions must
also be distinguished before admitting it. It contributes zero to this count.

## Technical evidence

Complete field/source mappings and public admission facts are saved in
[data/icons-road-28.json](../data/icons-road-28.json).

A310 uses the original Alpine Renault French road catalogue hosted by the Alpine
Renault forum as 1978. The 23-page PDF was downloaded and rendered in a contact
sheet; narrative PDF4/9 and technical leaf PDF22 were read individually. The
technical leaf explicitly specifies four synchronised forward gears, 2664-cc
90-degree light-alloy V6, one overhead cam per bank, 150 DIN ch at 6000 rpm,
20.8 mkg at 3500 rpm, 10:1 compression, a single-choke 34 TBIA and twin-choke
35 CEEI carburettor, four seats, polyester body/backbone construction,
independent suspension and ventilated front/solid rear discs. Native power and
torque units remain labelled. The host's 1978 attribution is not presented as
an independently authenticated print date. Later five-speed and revised rear
suspension data are excluded. Alpine's 2015 factory history supports the
sporting-GT transition; its narrative bhp notation does not replace the brochure.

A610 uses original Renault Communication pages dated 25 February 1991 and
embargoed to 19 March, announcing its Geneva introduction. The linked gallery's
cover, photographs and six text pages were downloaded; text pages 1–3 and
Annexe 2/page 5 were individually read. They establish 2975 cc, 250 ch at
5750 rpm, 35.7 mkg at 2900 rpm, 7.6:1 compression, multipoint injection,
Garrett T3, strengthened five-speed manual, rear-wheel drive, reinforced
backbone/platform, bonded composite panels, double-wishbone suspension and
ventilated discs with Bosch five-sensor ABS. The 2015 retrospective's differing
2963-cc/1990 wording is excluded from this launch specification. No conversion
of native units, tuned engine or race performance is introduced.

The A610 source URL is the HTTPS Alpine Renault forum index linking the exact
original dossier. Its linked scan gallery supports HTTP only; the gallery URL
is preserved explicitly in the source notes. The technical evidence is the
visually read Renault facsimile, not forum commentary. No certificate checks
were disabled. Factory history was readable through the web reader even where
local retrieval encountered certificate failure; failed routes are not cited
as technical evidence.

306 GTI-6 uses Peugeot's original UK Colours & Trims / Equipment & Options
technical insert, M/CP1243 February 1999, hosted by the 306 GTI-6 Owners Club.
The eight-page PDF was rendered in a contact sheet and PDF3/4/8 individually
read, including its dated footer. The GTI-6 column gives 1998 cc, four cylinders,
16 valves, native 167 BHP at 6500 rpm and 20.0 MKg at 5500 rpm, multipoint
injection, six-speed manual, three doors, disc brakes/ABS, power steering and
independent front strut/rear trailing-arm suspension. Rallye and other models
share some table columns; their differing mass and performance are not borrowed.
No bhp/PS/kW or torque conversion is invented.

The 31-page original UK range brochure M/CP1101 October 1998 was also rendered,
with GTI-6 PDF25–26 and footer PDF31 read individually. It supplies body and
six-speed road-model context, not a second mixed-year technical specification.
Peugeot's January 2026 factory heritage account independently supports the
Peugeot Sport gearbox and handling significance. Its broad 1993–2003 section
heading is not used as exact GTI-6 production chronology. No BTCC/Maxi power,
ACAV five-speed engine or Rallye specification is substituted.

bytes remain unchanged. SG2012's CC BY 2.0 1980 A310 V6 at Capesthorne Hall
represents the narrow early road body. Its sunroof, year and exterior do not

mechanical originality is inferred. FotoSleuth's CC BY 2.0 red 306 GTi-6 shows
the three-door road body and five-spoke wheels. Its current exhaust, equipment
and registration do not certify the February 1999 UK mechanical specification.
An alternative with visibly different wheels was not selected. Captions preserve
these limits. Creator, licence URL, Commons description page, credit and exact
SHA-256 asset hash are saved. Factory press images are not assumed openly licensed.
