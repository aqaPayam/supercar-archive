# Road-icon batch 138: Bugatti Type 43 road-touring family

Reviewed 2026-10-30 against all 934 existing identities. One absent Type 43
road programme brings the working inventory to 935 records: 540 curated road
identifiers across 138 manifests plus 395 preserved baseline records. This is
not a certified qualifying count toward the 1,000-icon goal.

## Admission and identity

The Type 43 belongs for combining Grand Prix-derived supercharged engineering
with touring accommodation, and its established place among early high-performance
road sports cars. Age and rarity alone are not admission evidence.
The full inventory was checked for Type 43/43A, T43 and Grand Sport touring
aliases. No Type 43 road family was present. Group its engines, years, bodies
and individual chassis once; dedicated racers, replicas and modified Type 43/44
combinations receive no additional admission.

The existing Type 55 is a separately named later road programme. Its original
[factory history](https://newsroom.bugatti.com/press-releases/bugatti-super-sport-legends)
was checked in the relevant Type 55 section and expressly compares it with
Type 43. A similar quoted engine size does not make the two names aliases;
no later Type 55 specification or performance is transferred here.

## Selected museum technical reference

[The Louwman Museum entry](https://www.louwmanmuseum.nl/en/collections/automobiles/bugatti-type-43)
was read directly in complete relevant scope. The selected reference is its
1929 Type 43 touring car. All six technical attributes use that description:
eight cylinders, quoted 2.3 L, supercharging, three valves per cylinder with
two intake and one exhaust, eight-spoke alloy wheels and steering-linked
headlamps. The lighting detail describes this museum car, not universal original
factory equipment. No precise displacement, inline layout, output, rpm, torque,
gearbox, seats, mass, dimensions or measured performance is inferred.

Earlier search metadata labelled bodywork racecar; the direct current entry
exposes no such field and describes touring bodywork and a production car.
Public-sale road identity is independently checked against the Trust account
below. The following Type 35 racing-history paragraph is kept separate:
its race totals do not become Type 43 achievements. Exclusive first-160-km/h
priority and the top-gear pull-away claim are not imported as technical fields.

## Public road identity and historical limits

[The Bugatti Trust's archive-custodian account](https://www.bugatti-trust.co.uk/sound-recording-of-4-bugatti-engines/)
was read, including its Type 43 section accompanying a 1977 CAR GRAPHIC book
and record. It identifies the Type 43 among early fast sports cars sold to the
public. Its restored 1927 chassis 43208 is a different reference: its approximately
120-bhp figure, seats, totals, owner story and whereabouts are not copied into
the selected technical fields. Neither the audio nor original Japanese book
pages were inspected. No exclusive world-first priority is asserted.

[Gordon Cruickshank's original February 2015 Motor Sport feature](https://www.motorsportmagazine.com/archive/article/february-2015/104/phoenix-flames/)
was read via direct page text. It distinguishes the touring-road programme
from Grand Prix cars and investigates Campbell's different chassis 43171,
its racing fire, road registration, altered components and restoration. This
is historical investigation, not a new stock-road performance test. Type 44
axle, nonstandard fuel tank, quoted owner speeds, individual originality and
registration details do not describe the selected Louwman car.

## Licensed representative photograph

[AlfvanBeem's own-work image](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:1929_Bugatti_Type_43_p1.JPG)
was photographed at Louwman Museum on 6 March 2011. Summary, file history,
API metadata and [CC0 1.0 Universal dedication](http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/deed.en)
were read. The 1280 x 960 JPEG rendition was individually viewed and copied
unchanged. Blue touring body, upright screen, wings and eight-spoke wheels
match the intended family/body. Category chassis 43-268 is context, not
photographic authentication of serial, engine, year or equipment originality.
