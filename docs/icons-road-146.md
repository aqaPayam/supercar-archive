## Admission and identity

The Apollo combines Buick engineering with bespoke Italian coachwork shaped
by Ron Plescia and Franco Scaglione. Martin Buckley's original drive and
interview with founder Milt Brown document a coherent sporting GT project.
This supports a performance-history admission beyond rarity alone.

The fresh complete 942-record identity inventory was checked for Apollo,
Vetta, Ventura, International Motor and Intermeccanica. Its only hits were
modern Gumpert Apollo and the later Griffith/Omega/Torino/Italia development.
The manufacturer history separates Apollo from that later project. Neither
is an alias collision. Group original Apollo/Vetta Ventura road production
once conservatively; exclude GT.L 2+2 and mid-engine proposals, dedicated
racers, replicas and additional bodies/engines. Variant/racer boundary
questions remain pending without being reasked.
