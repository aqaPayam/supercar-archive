## Admission and duplicate review

The D8 combines established sporting straight-eight heritage with the French
grand-touring tradition. The [Delage Register's road-car history](https://www.delageregister.co.uk/)
identifies the D8 and its Super Sports version; the
[selected museum record](https://toyota-automobile-museum.jp/en/archives/car-database/detail.html?id=333)
identifies the later D8-120 as a high-performance model. The admission case
does not rest on price, rarity, celebrity ownership or beauty alone.

A fresh complete 939-record identity inventory was checked for Delage,
D8/D8S, D8-100/120/15 and spacing aliases. No existing road family was found.
Historic road engines, bodies, years and individual chassis are grouped
conservatively as one D8 family. Dedicated GP/record machines, replicas and
individual coachwork do not create extra additions. This does not certify
that every D8 derivative is mechanically identical or equally sporting.
