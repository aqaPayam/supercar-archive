# Squire production-road reference hold

Reviewed 30 October 2026 against the complete 944-record identity inventory.
No Squire entry was found. No record, technical field, image or qualifying
count is added by this research hold.

The [Simeone collection page](https://simeonemuseum.org/collection/1933-squire-roadster/)
identifies chassis X101 and labels the reference as a 1933 Squire Roadster.
Its relevant collection text was read through the history and Our Car
sections. The museum explicitly calls its own car “the prototype”. Although
it describes actual road use, that does not remove the prototype boundary.
A production-road reference should be established before using this car's
specifications for a new entry under the current conservative scope.

The supercharged Anzani engine and preselector gearbox make the family a
promising sporting candidate, but neither rarity nor collector praise is
an admission by itself. Museum performance superlatives and production
totals were not independently verified or imported. No original road test,
factory brochure or licensed representative image was reviewed for import.

Keep X101 out pending a documented original production-road reference or an
explicit decision to admit historically significant prototypes. Do not reask
the existing scope question or count this hold toward the 1,000-icon goal.
This does not reject the entire Squire road family, authenticate its exact
build date or certify any replica or later continuation.
