## Admission and identity

The early Impala SS 409 is an American performance and cultural icon: the optional dual-quad big-block paired serious factory road-car hardware with the Super Sport coupe, while the 409 name became part of popular music. Chevrolet’s history explicitly connects the early SS programme with the 1962 Beach Boys song.

Complete identifier/manufacturer/family/generation/variant search covered
Impala, Biscayne, Bel Air and 409 references. Only the separate 1994–1996
Impala SS was present. Count the 1961–1964 performance-road family once using
the selected 1962 SS Sport Coupe, optional RPO 587 engine and four-speed.
Years, engines, SS trim and bodies do not multiply the count. Race-prepared
cars, lightweight drag specials, replicas and Z11 hardware are excluded.

## Evidence boundaries

The [original 409 factory sheets](https://over-drive-magazine.com/wp-content/uploads/Chevrolet/1962/1962-Chevrolet-Factory-W-Series-Specs-1-10.pdf)
are a 4,306,282-byte ten-page extract. Full PDF indexes 1–5 and 8 were visually
read: identification, ratings, model-1669 calculation table, curves, components
and lubrication/cooling. The ratings leaf is revised January 1962; component
and cooling leaves carry April revisions. Other pages were thumbnail navigation
only, not independently audited. The 126-page mirror was discovered but not
locally downloaded or visually reviewed.

The selected dual-carb RPO 587 is separated from single-carb RPO 580. Explicit
gross power/torque conventions are retained without modern net or kW conversion.
The model-1669 performance-weight table includes a passenger allowance and
is not this coupe's curb weight. Its calculated axle examples are not assigned
as installed ratios. No measured acceleration, maximum speed or race tune is
imported, and the earlier identification leaf does not establish a later
engine suffix.

The [full-size road brochure](https://over-drive-magazine.com/wp-content/uploads/Chevrolet/1962/1962-Chevrolet-Full-Size-Brochure-1-16C.pdf)
is an 11,915,921-byte nine-page scan. Full index 3 was visually read: the SS
panel names Sport Coupe/convertible availability, bucket seats/locking console,
optional engines through 409 hp and four-speed Synchro-Mesh. Index 8 was
rendered but not individually read; other pages were thumbnail navigation only.
SS trim alone does not imply a 409, standard Positraction or every heavy-duty
option. Secondary option-price summaries are not specification authority.

[Chevrolet's legacy account](https://www.chevrolet.com/legacy/impala), specifically
the 1961–1964 section, supports the SS introduction and song connection.
No lyrics, general model sales totals or unverified song/movie statistics are
reproduced. Exact field/source mappings are in the
[reviewed manifest](../data/icons-road-104.json).
