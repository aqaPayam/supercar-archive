The RS road car’s 220-PS boxer, manual transmission and performance chassis connect that engineering history to a production saloon. ## S401 STI version GH-BES six-speed manual — 2002 Japan reference

The S401 represents the third-generation Legacy’s STI-developed performance pinnacle: a hand-balanced turbo boxer, six-speed manual, front limited-slip differential and substantial steering, suspension and brake revisions. Those changes make it a distinct performance reference within the Legacy road lineage. Count the absent third generation once, including B4/Liberty, RSK/GT-B, sedan/wagon and S401 identities; the special edition does not create an extra count alongside its base family.

as February 1989. The technical evidence is the manufacturer scan itself,
not the owner's restoration commentary or modern spec compilations. The actual
large final spread (11.JPG, printed pages 18–19) was visually inspected, including
enlarged crops of its specification and equipment columns for legibility.
It labels the selected car RS, E-BC5 and 5MT. Clear fields are EJ20-TURBO,
horizontally opposed four-cylinder DOHC turbo, 1994 cc, 92 × 75 mm, compression
8.5, 220 PS at 6400 rpm, 27.5 kg·m at 4000 rpm, EGI multi-point injection,
independent front/rear struts, and ventilated discs front/rear. Its equipment
column explicitly lists full-time 4WD. Native advertised units are preserved;
an unread small-print rating convention, exact engine suffix or conversion
is not supplied. Dimensions, masses, tyres, ratios, LSD hardware and options
are not imported merely to enlarge the specification list.

The final-spread preview and preceding interior spread preview were viewed,
and the actual large back cover (02.JPG) was viewed for manufacturer identity.
Large cover, printed interior and other preceding spread files were downloaded
but not all individually inspected. Downloading all previews is not a full
brochure audit. One intermediate image alt label refers to the January general
sedan catalogue; it is not used to redate or certify the whole scanned set.
Only the explicitly identified RS table and equipment column supply fields.
The cropped source-review images remain temporary analysis material; they are
not website photographs or altered manufacturer specifications.

Subaru's actual historical account independently repeats the RS output/torque
and separates the detuned 200-PS automatic GT. It says the three endurance
