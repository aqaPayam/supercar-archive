## Identity and admission review

The full 806-record inventory was searched across manufacturer, family, generation
and variant, including W113/Pagoda, C219/CLS, Cobalt and Falcon/FPV aliases. Only
the earlier XY GT-HO Falcon was present among these families/generations. The
original W202 C36 and W203 C55 AMG were also found during gap research; they are
not added again. The R230 SL65 Black Series, W121 190 SL, BMW 328 and BMW 507
remain existing entries. The W113 is distinct from those SL generations. One
Cobalt family is represented by its turbo sedan; a coupe, supercharged engine or
naturally aspirated SS does not earn another entry. FPV branding does not conceal
its Ford Falcon lineage, and the selected FG is distinct from the archived XY.

## Technical evidence boundaries

The W113 uses Mercedes-Benz's factory 60-year retrospective identifying the
March 1963 230 SL. It supplies the M127 six-cylinder, 2306 cc, 110 kW, two-seat
roadster, front discs, optional concave removable hardtop and reinforced safety
structure. The first-sports-car safety claim is not repeated universally. The
W111-derived shortened/reinforced floor is distinguished from a wholly unrelated
chassis. Later 250/280 engines, rear discs and California rear bench are excluded.
No inferred standard automatic or gearbox-specific acceleration is imported.
Its significance combines design and sporting-road safety, not an ordinary
low-output luxury trim or a transplanted rally-car tune.

The CLS 55 uses the original Mercedes-Benz USA 2006 brochure, PDF page 3's own
AMG column and page 14's engine/transmission account, both rendered and visually
read. It preserves 5439-cc SOHC 24-valve V8, helical supercharger/intercooler,
469 net US hp, 516 lb-ft and five-speed AMG automatic. CLS 500's 302 hp and
seven-speed gearbox are not transferred. The selected US model-year reference
notes delayed availability and does not assert the global introduction date.
Optional equipment, speedometer scale and measured acceleration are not inferred.
The modern four-door-coupe design case does not claim that Mercedes invented
every earlier four-door coupe worldwide.

The Cobalt uses its July 2008-printed 2009 US factory brochure, whose SS sections
were visually read because text extraction corrupts some fonts. The 2.0-litre
DOHC turbo engine, 260 hp, Performance Handling suspension, four-wheel disc ABS
and Brembo four-piston front brakes apply to SS Turbocharged. The limited-slip
differential and Performance Display are available equipment. The ordinary
2.2-litre engine and Sport Appearance Package are not SS turbo specifications.
A contemporary Car and Driver driving report explicitly shares the coupe/sedan
powertrain treatment and supplies direct injection, 260 lb-ft, five-speed manual
and front-wheel drive. It supports the chassis/Lightning Lap admission case,
while criticising interior quality and seating adjustment. Sedan measured times
are not transplanted to the coupe, and the magazine's front-drive track result
is not a universal lap record. Only this one turbo-sedan reference is counted.

The FG F6 uses Ford Performance Vehicles' January 2009 brochure, with its own
sedan column read in the rendered matrix. The 4.0-litre DOHC 24-valve turbo six,
310 DIN kW, 565 Nm, TR6060 manual, limited-slip differential and independent
sedan rear suspension are retained. The torque figure carries its 98 RON
qualification. The standard four-piston-front brake package is distinct from
optional premium brakes. The no-cost ZF automatic, ute rear suspension, F6X
270-kW/AWD SUV and later F6 E/Mk II equipment are excluded. First-hand June 2008
New Zealand launch driving supports response and turn-in, while noting visibility
and intercooler-protection concerns. Manufacturer wheel-power comparisons,
unqualified acceleration and New Zealand prices are not adopted as measured
Australian facts.

Several candidate brochure hosts returned access or certificate errors. No TLS
verification was disabled or identity/locale changed to bypass them. Readable
original US CLS, US Cobalt and Australian FPV brochures from other public archives
supply the selected references; no unavailable PDF or search snippet substitutes
for a read specification column. The factory Pagoda account was read through
the public web reader.
