## Duplicate and scope review

The full inventory was checked before selection. Each M5 is a different
previously absent generation, with one road reference per generation.
The first-generation F-series RS and original G-series 930 are distinct
from each other and from the existing 964/993/996/later 911 entries.
Clio I Williams is distinct from the existing mid-engined Clio II V6.
No new paint editions, additional body styles or second engine tunes
within these selected generations are counted.

`data/icons-road-05.json` carries the sourced public admission reasons,
attribute references, exact qualifications, photograph provenance and
SHA-256 digests. Technical evidence is from the manufacturers, including
Porsche as the commissioned developer/assembler of the 500 E.

## Important evidence boundaries

- M5: separate E28 European M88/3, E34 original S38B36 3.5, E39 S62 and
  E60 V10/SMG. Later 3.8/six-speed/Touring and North American engine or
  transmission choices are not blended. E60's F1 connection is a shared
  foundry, not a claim that its engine was a Formula 1 engine.
- M635CSi: original 210-kW non-catalyst specification, not lower-output M6.
  Z3 M Coupe: European S50B32, not US S52 or later S54. The Z3 article's
  3,153-cc typo and conflicting 1,270/1,465-kg weights are withheld; the
  consistent 3.2-litre engine narrative is used. An AI-labelled BMW Classic
  page is not used as technical evidence.
- CSL: final 3,153-cc road car, not earlier capacities or racing output.
  Manufacturer says the large rear wing was delivered in the boot and was
  not street-legal in the described German configuration. The photograph's
  mounted display wing is explicitly qualified in its public caption.
- Mercedes: production gullwing, not W194 racer/roadster/replica; original
  500 E, not later E 500 Limited or Brabus; road 6.3, not Red Pig or a
  Crayford estate. Configuration-dependent 300 SL top speed is withheld.
- Speedster: original steel-bodied production Pre-A 1500, not the
  aluminium America Roadster, museum prototype or four-cam Carrera. Their
  power figures are not transferred. The underlying production 356 engine
  layout has a separate manufacturer reference.
- RS 2.7: M472 Touring, using its 240-km/h and 6.3-second figures rather
  than lighter M471 Sport performance. Its 154.5 kW is calculated from
  210 PS at 0.73549875 kW/PS and identified as such in the source notes.
- 930: European 1975 production 3.0, not prototype 2.7, US 1976 output or
  intercooled 3.3. 944: the museum's 1989 standard 250-PS manual road car,
  not earlier 220-PS, Turbo S trim, Cabriolet or Cup race specification.
  Both one-page museum PDFs were rendered and inspected in full.
- 924: Carrera GT, not GTS/GTR or body-kit Turbo. 928: original 4.5-litre
  European engine, not later S/S4/GT/GTS or lower-output market versions.
- GTA: standard road homologation, not Corsa/SA/GTAm racing figures.
  GTV6: 2.5 road architecture; an inconsistent retrospective output table
  is withheld. SZ: production coupe, not the different Balocco prototype
  on a Heritage sales page; contradictory output and undefined weight
  figures are withheld. Montreal: production 2.6 V8, not the 1967
  four-cylinder show car whose data appears on another Heritage page.
- GTI: original 1.6/110-PS concept; a conflicting cc figure in a related
  museum document is withheld. Williams: production begins in 1993, not
  the earlier date in a photo filename. English manufacturer hp notation
  is retained without an assumed PS conversion.

Unqualified weights, racing performance and unsupported options remain
unfilled. Missing numbers are preferable to a misleadingly complete profile.

## Photographs

All 22 selected photographs were visually reviewed for the intended model
and body. Initial results included wrong-generation M5s, Mercedes roadsters,
a 300 SL replica, a Brabus W124, Crayford estates, Speedster prototypes,
racing GTAs and later Golfs. These were replaced. The GTA road-bodied
example was photographed at Balocco; its installed engine is not certified
by the image. The 928 is an original-body example with an open bonnet.

Representative photographs may show another year, market, wheels or event
equipment. Their captions do not certify factory identity or installed
engine. Creators, original file pages, licences and unaltered image bytes
are retained; no manufacturer publicity image is assumed freely licensed.
