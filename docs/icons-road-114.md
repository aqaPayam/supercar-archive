## Admission and identity

The Shelby CSX brought unusual turbocharger and lightweight-wheel engineering to the American front-drive performance hatch. The contemporary Motor Trend road test documents its variable-nozzle turbo, composite wheels and sharp handling, alongside noise, torque steer and brake-fade limitations. The 1989 VNT represents the original CSX road programme once, rather than supplying separate entries for its model years.

Complete identities were checked for CSX, Shadow and Sundance aliases, plus
every Shelby identity. No target was present. Count the 1987–1989 Shadow-based
CSX programme once across years and packages. Cobra CSX chassis numbers,
Omni GLHS, CSX-T rental specifications, prototypes and engine swaps are not
substituted for this selected 1989 VNT road reference.

## Technical evidence and limits

The [original May 1989 Motor Trend test](https://www.flickr.com/photos/triggerscarstuff/albums/72157628296484915/)
by Jeff Karr was visually read in all four hosted scans, printed pages 162–166.
It supports the turbo engineering, composite wheels and handling significance,
with acknowledged harshness, torque steer and brake-fade limitations.
Twenty-three attributes map to this test, including its optional 225/50VR15
fitment and Recaro seats; they are not represented as universal standard kit.
Getrag brand is documented, but no gearbox model number is assigned.

The test body and technical table disagree on torque peak (2400 versus what
appears to be 3400 rpm); torque and its peak are unassigned. The 175-hp SAE-net
power at 5200 rpm is consistent. No unqualified world-first VNT/composite-wheel
claim, production total, top speed or acceleration is imported. The later 2020
Motor Trend retrospective supplies no controlling technical field.

The [Shelby owner supplement](https://www.shelbyregistry.com/1989csx-ownersmanual-1.html)
was reviewed as a cover scan and linked text transcriptions -2 through -6,
not a full paginated scan. Its 14-US-gallon fuel capacity is the remaining
attribute. Its gross vehicle weight is not used as curb weight. The registry
summary does not override the period test. The unique-parts PDF was inaccessible,
and the official Shelby history redirected to an unavailable page; neither is
claimed as read evidence. Wikipedia was navigation only. Discovered C&D and
Road & Track references were not read and supply no imported fields.

Exact source mappings and limitations are retained in the
[manifest](../data/icons-road-114.json).
