## Admission and identity

The original step-down Hudson Hornet is a historic American performance icon: its low-set chassis and large six-cylinder engine underpinned the Fabulous Hudson Hornet stock-car programme, whose success and enduring cultural recognition extend well beyond the car’s rarity. This production-road representative preserves that lineage without adopting racing hardware.

The complete manufacturer, model, generation, variant and identifier inventory
was searched again for Hudson, Hornet, Twin H/Twin-H and step-down spellings.
No target was present. This is the original 1951–1954 step-down family, using
one 1954 US production-road reference. Body choices, years, standard/Twin H
engines and transmission options do not multiply its count. No later AMC
Hornet, restored NASCAR racer, 7X-equipped competition car or replica is added.
Jensen FF was excluded from new candidates after confirming its batch-29 record.
The existing Hudson research hold is updated, rather than appended again.
