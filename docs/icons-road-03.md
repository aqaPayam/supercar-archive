## Specification boundaries

- Shelby: original road fastback, not an R-model, Hertz car or continuation.
  The museum's later automatic/colour statements are excluded.
- GNX: named production hardware only. Unqualified fastest-car claims,
  experimental development tuning and the retrospective's T-top anecdote
  are not treated as factory GNX specifications.
- AMX: the museum's incorrect 289.6-cu-in engine table and inconsistent
  output/torque figures are excluded. FIA PDF page 17 identifies the 1970
  390 at 6390.9 cc; page 24 gives compression. Optional competition parts
  elsewhere in that form are not assigned to the road car.
- Trans Am: 1974 road automatic. Conflicting torque-peak RPM and test
  performance without conditions are withheld.
- Chevelle: GM kit PDF pages 130-131, AMA pages 3-4 dated 15 October 1969,
  distinguish LS6 from LS5 and the experimental LS7. The four-speed sport
  coupe is the selected configuration. A museum's replacement-engine
  custom Chevelle was rejected as factory specification evidence.
- Camaro: GM kit PDF pages 92-93, AMA pages 3-4 dated 1 November 1968,
  identify the 1969 Z/28 road 302, 11:1 compression and four-speed choices.
  Optional cross-ram induction and four-wheel discs are not assumed.
- Oldsmobile: GM kit PDF pages 2-3 and 10 identify W-30 equipment. Manual
  coupe selected; W-31, Hurst/Olds and optional aluminium axle/spoiler
  equipment are excluded.

Historical advertised power is explicitly qualified and is not converted
to an unqualified modern power-filter value. Missing values remain missing.
Period PDF pages were rendered and inspected; OCR only assisted navigation.

All seven Commons photographs were visually reviewed for the intended
generation/body and obvious racing or restomod substitutions. They are
representative exteriors, not certifications of an installed engine or
factory identity. Original bytes, creators, licences and SHA-256 digests
are retained. No manufacturer publicity image is assumed to be freely
licensed.

## Validation

The regression suite replays every curated manifest, checks collisions,
preserves the baseline and verifies repeat safety and transaction rollback.
Import/export checks also compare every pre-existing database row, SQLite
integrity and foreign keys, public admission facts, image checksums and
local/static data parity. Startup preservation is checked on the resulting
database. Browser automation remains unavailable, so no new interactive
browser test is claimed.
