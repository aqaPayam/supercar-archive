The 1,000-qualifying-icon goal and broader baseline eligibility audit remain

## Admission and identity

The original Riviera Gran Sport earns a place as an American performance-GT icon by combining the distinctive first-generation Riviera with a dual-quad Super Wildcat engine, positive-traction axle and purposeful factory performance package. Its contemporary first-hand evaluation supports the driving case as well as recording handling limitations.

The complete identifier/manufacturer/model/generation/variant inventory was
checked for Riviera, XP-715 and related first-generation identities. No target
was present. Existing Buick entries are Regal GNX and GSX Stage 1, separate
programmes. Count the original 1963–1965 Riviera family once using this 1965
Gran Sport road reference; standard engines, dual-quad options, years and
handling equipment do not supply additional entries. Later-generation
Rivieras, concepts, replicas and dedicated racers are not admitted here.

## Sources and specification limits

The [Buick ’65 factory catalogue](https://xr793.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/1965-Buick-Full-Line-Prestige-R.pdf)
is a 34,301,542-byte, 26-page scanned PDF. Full PDF index 4 (printed 5) and
index 23 (printed 42–43) were visually read. The adjacent Special/Skylark
specification leaf at index 24 was also viewed, and its figures were excluded.
A 12-page thumbnail contact sheet was navigation only; the remaining catalogue
pages were not individually audited.

The Gran Sport page overrides ordinary Riviera engine and axle entries.
Advertised power, native units and factory option qualifications are retained.
In particular, firmer suspension and faster steering are separate available
equipment, rather than automatic Gran Sport contents. No gross/net conversion,
estimated maximum speed, test time, curb weight or production total is imported.

The [June 1965 first-hand road report](https://www.caranddriver.com/reviews/a64253059/1965-buick-riviera-gran-sport-archive-test/)
supports admission, V8 configuration and the three-speed automatic. Its
handling criticism is not erased. The repost's inconsistent metric and
quarter-mile fields are excluded. Exact mappings are in the
[reviewed manifest](../data/icons-road-102.json).

The [Free Library's preserved Buick PR caption](https://libwww.freelibrary.org/digital/item/29880)
was also read for period identity context; its photograph is not reused.
The obsolete www-prefixed catalogue host failed resolution; the public
non-www host supplied the complete scan. No access controls were bypassed.
