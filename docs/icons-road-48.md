## Evidence boundaries

- **Scirocco Mk1 GTI:** Volkswagen's own heritage catalogue establishes the
  Giugiaro 2+2, front-wheel drive, GTI's 1976 introduction, 81 kW / 110 PS and
  185 km/h. The original factory 2008 chronology distinguishes the street GTI's
  Bosch K-Jetronic from Junior Cup racing cars. No inferred displacement,
  transmission or disputed production-end year is added.
- **Scirocco Mk3 R:** Original Volkswagen UK December 2011 brochure, printed
  pages 42–45, rendered and visually read. The R 265-PS column establishes
  1984 cc, 195 kW, 350 Nm and six-speed manual; 0–62 mph is 6.0 seconds for
  that manual. The technology page confirms direct injection, four valves per
  cylinder, turbocharging, front drive and electronic XDS. The DSG's different
  acceleration, Golf R all-wheel drive, later 280-PS facelift and R-Cup racer
  are not transferred.
- **RS 6 C7/C8:** The original German Audi factory 25 July 2022 retrospective
  was read by generation. C7 uses its original four-litre V8, 700 Nm,
  eight-speed tiptronic and standard adaptive air suspension. No horsepower
  value is calculated from the article's relative comparison. The C8 reference
  is European introduction in 2019: four litres, 600 PS, 800 Nm, 48-volt
  assistance and four-wheel steering. Its section does not explicitly establish
  a gearbox, so none is transferred from C7. US model-year availability, later
  Performance power and package-dependent maximum speeds are excluded.
- **RC F:** Lexus Canada's original 2015 model announcement and November 2014
  specification-sheet section were read separately from the preliminary PDF
  insert. Physical page 7 was rendered and read for DOHC, 32 valves and D-4S.
  The announcement establishes V8, eight-speed automatic, rear drive, four
  seats, independent suspension and Brembo discs. Preliminary output is
  omitted. The optional Performance Package TVD is not presented as standard.
  Malformed torque-unit and cabin-dimension fields are not imported.
- **GS F:** Original Lexus US 2016-model engineering release, containing the
  October 2015 launch account, establishes the naturally aspirated five-litre
  V8, 467 hp, 389 lb-ft, eight-speed SPDS and 12.3:1 compression. Native US
  units remain. Disputed valve-material wording, optional-equipment ambiguity
  and later chassis changes are excluded.
