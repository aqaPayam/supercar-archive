# Peugeot 104 ZS2 — batch 84

Reviewed 30 October 2026. One original Peugeot 104 sporting-road family is
admitted through the 1979 ZS2 homologation reference, not every ZS engine,
gearbox, badge, year or later competition evolution.

## Admission and identity

The 104 ZS2 was Peugeot’s purpose-built Group 2 homologation special, rather than an ordinary 104 with cosmetic trim. Its enlarged engine, twin Solex carburettors and documented production recognition mark an early step in Peugeot’s sporting-hatchback history before the 205 GTI. One original 104 sporting family is admitted; other ZS engines, later gearboxes and competition evolutions do not add separate counts.

The complete 867-car identity inventory was searched for 104, ZS2, ZS 2,
104C91, 104 C91, 51A, Samba and Citroen/Citroën LN/LNA aliases. No 104,
ZS2, C91 or LN/LNA identity was present. The only incidental “104” matches
were the Lotus Carlton Type 104 and an Austin-Healey brochure number, neither
a Peugeot. The Samba Rallye is already present, but the original Peugeot
short three-door 104 is a distinct model/body programme with its own earlier
sporting and homologation history; shared engine/platform ancestry alone does
not make it an alias for the longer, differently bodied Samba. The actual base
forms were compared: 104 form 1696 page 1 records a 2230-mm wheelbase and
3315-mm length including bumpers; [Samba A-5157 page 2](https://historicdb.fia.com/sites/default/files/car_attachment/1601066701/homologation_form_number_5157_group_a.pdf)
records 2340 mm and 3506 mm. These dimensions support the body/programme
distinction without claiming unrelated engineering. No additional
Samba, ordinary 104 saloon or Citroën badge is being admitted.

The basis is the ZS2's documented sporting/homologation purpose and place in
Peugeot's early hot-hatch history. Age, rarity and the existence of a photo do
not by themselves qualify an ordinary 104. Peugeot's manufacturer account and
the original manufacturer recognition provide independent roles: the former
establishes sporting-road context, the latter bounds the selected mechanics.

rollcage, dashboard, seat or tank extensions are not imported. Later pages and
