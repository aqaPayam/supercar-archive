## Admission and identity

The Legacy GT brought WRX-like turbo performance to Subaru's understated
all-weather grand tourer. Its contemporary sporting-GT appeal is documented
alongside its limitations, rather than treated as proof of a flawless sports
sedan. The generation also earned Subaru's first Japan Car of the Year award;
that range-wide recognition is separate from certification of the Canadian car.

The full inventory was checked for Legacy/Liberty and relevant BL/BP, S402,
Spec.B and Outback names. Only first-generation BC/BF and third-generation
BE/BH S401 road families are present. The new generation is absent.
Sedan/wagon, B4/Liberty names, engines, Spec.B/STI derivatives, years and
related fourth-generation Outback versions remain within this single family.

Subaru's [March 2005 chronology](https://www.subaru.co.jp/en/news/archives/press/2005/05_03_15e.html)
was also read in its relevant body and timeline. It explicitly identifies the
fourth-generation launch in May 2003 and includes Outback in the Legacy totals.
This supports the identity grouping; no production totals or speed-record
figures from other generations are imported.

## Exact selected reference

The selected car is a **2005 Canadian Legacy 2.5GT Limited manual sedan**.
The [original Subaru Canada brochure](https://www.centuryu.com/uploads/1/4/4/6/144698822/2005_legacy.pdf)
is SBL05E, printed in Canada in 2004. Its cover identifies subaru.ca and
Canadian publication. All 20 technical attributes map to its printed pp. 44–45
specification/equipment spread at PDF index 22. Cover/back-cover index 0 and
final spreads 22–23 were individually inspected as rendered images. Several
other spreads were rendered for navigation; no full 48-page visual audit is claimed.

The GT Limited **sedan/manual** mass column and manual viscous centre
differential are selected. The automatic's VTD system, wagon mass, ordinary
SOHC engine and other grades' equipment are not combined with this reference.
Displacement remains nominal 2.5 litres; no exact cc or engine-code suffix is
inferred. Japanese twin-scroll technology, a Japanese weight-reduction claim,
US test figures and aftermarket modifications supply no Canadian attributes.

A separately downloaded two-face foldout is image-only and was not visually
read; the complete Canadian brochure controls. The search-discovered US PDF
returned 403 through both web and ordinary HTTP and supplies no specifications.

## Contemporary evidence and limits

The [original 2003 award release](https://www.subaru.co.jp/en/news/archives/press/2003/03_11_13e.htm)
was read in full. The award applies to the new Legacy range, not specifically
the 2005 Canadian manual sedan or a motorsport achievement.

The [2006 long-term production-car test](https://www.caranddriver.com/reviews/a15386001/2005-subaru-legacy-25gt-limited-long-term-road-test/)
was read through the relevant stock-car body and separate aftermarket section.
It records sleeper appeal and practical performance, with chassis and ownership
limits. Optional shifter, individual-car faults, driver misfuelling and dealer
installation damage are distinguished. Modified-car results are not factory facts.

The [October 2004 comparison](https://www.caranddriver.com/reviews/comparison-test/a15132073/2004-acura-tsx-vs-audi-a4-vs-subaru-legacy-vs-volvo-s40-comparison-test/)
was read in its introduction and complete Legacy section/panel. Third overall
is not a class win. Its displayed 2004 test-car label and US measured figures
do not define this Canadian reference. Exact mappings and qualifications are
retained in the [manifest](../data/icons-road-126.json).
