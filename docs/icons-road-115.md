## Admission and identity

The i30 N established Hyundai’s N performance-car identity. Its first-hand contemporary road review supports the significance of the adjustable chassis, traction and engaging handling, while acknowledging turbo lag, torque steer, firm ride and noise. The original manual Performance hatchback represents the road family once across outputs, bodies, years and facelift changes.

The complete identity inventory was checked for i30 N/i30N, Elantra and
Veloster aliases, plus every Hyundai identity. Only personal Verna was present.
Count the original PD-based road N family once across outputs, years, hatch,
Fastback and facelift. No ordinary i30, Elantra GT, N Line, TCR, Project C,
Veloster N or later 280-PS/DCT technical substitution.

on 19-inch wheels. Acceleration and speed remain factory claims. Curb-mass
ranges, luggage capacity without stiffness bar, prices and emissions are omitted.
No later forged wheels, brake sizes, DCT or facelift torque are imported.

was read in its relevant full body and specification panel. Its chassis praise
and drawbacks support admission; its performance and mass estimates are not
