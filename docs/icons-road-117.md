## Admission and identity

The i20 N earned both Top Gear’s 2021 Performance Car of the Year and overall Car of the Year awards. The award publisher’s assessment establishes the small hot hatch’s exceptional road-and-track appeal beyond its modest size and price. This original six-speed-manual road family is represented once; WRC inspiration is not a claim that the road car shares rally-car hardware or results.

Complete identities were checked for i20 and every Hyundai identity. Only
personal Verna, i30 N and Veloster N were present. Count the original BC3-based
N road family once across years and equipment; Indian BI3 N Line, ordinary i20
and WRC/Rally1/Rally2 competition cars are not this technical reference.

## Evidence and boundaries

The [original UK specification sheet](https://www.hyundai.news/newsroom/dam/eu/uk/20210617_i20_n_pricing_specs/hyundai-uk-i20-n-technical-data-0621.pdf)
is dated 16 June 2021 (filename path 17 June). Its 162,677-byte, three-page PDF
was downloaded; all three pages were individually visually read. Twenty-six
attributes map to the two technical-table pages. The header confirms standard
N Corner Carving Differential, Launch Control and rev matching. Normal and
temporary-overboost torque retain their distinct operating ranges. PS is not
mislabeled as hp. No single curb mass, price or measured performance is assigned.

The [original factory introduction](https://www.hyundai.news/uk/press-kit-archive/i20-n-2021/hyundai-motor-unveils-the-all-new-hyundai-i20-n.html)
was read for model, engine, chassis and European road-launch context. It describes
column-mounted C-MDPS, while the UK sheet lists R-MDPS. It also gives 6.7 seconds
0–100 km/h versus the UK sheet’s 6.2 seconds 0–62 mph. Steering assistance
location/ratio and acceleration are withheld rather than silently reconciled.
The UK 5.27-m turning-circle label is ambiguous and is also omitted. No WRC
minimum-weight equivalence, rally hardware or racing result is assigned to the
road car. Earlier general-market optional equipment does not override the UK
standard-equipment statement.

[Top Gear’s own award announcement](https://www.topgear.com/car-news/revealed-winners-2021-topgearcom-awards)
was read in both relevant sections. It names i20 N for Performance Car of the
Year and overall Car of the Year 2021. This is bounded evidence of recognition,
not approval of every ordinary derivative or proof of universal superiority.
The exact mappings and omissions remain in the [manifest](../data/icons-road-117.json).
