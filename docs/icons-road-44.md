## Primary evidence and limits

- **Impala:** The actual GM PDF physical pages 3–4 (September 1993 order guide)
  and 6 (14 February 1994 production announcement) were rendered and read.
  They establish production hardware and P255/50ZR17 tyres. The archive's early
  driving preview, 225-section tyre claim, estimated acceleration and unrelated
  Caprice/Fleetwood/Corvette specification columns are excluded. Engine sheets
  on pages 8–9 were inspected but not used to transfer other applications' data.
- **Accord:** Public indexed text of Honda's original Geneva 2001 release was
  read, including the Accord update and Type-R paragraphs. Direct retrieval of
  the release page was unavailable; no access restriction was bypassed. The
  separately accessible Honda UK historical account was cross-read. No Civic
  i-VTEC/VTC, 2.3 ES gearing, five-door Type-R or secondary specification table
  is transferred. Unestablished cc, rpm, mass and ratios are omitted.
- **SVX:** The linked full-resolution scan archive was publicly offered free by
  its host. Actual spread 18 was read at legible resolution; the 400-pixel
  preview alone was inadequate. Select US 230-HP and Active Torque-Split
  automatic hardware. Omit the conflicting printed 3317-cc figure, Japanese
  240-PS output, inferred VTD torque split, later FWD grades and modern racing
  EG33 derivatives.
- **Vitesse:** The museum's own 1986 record is the reference. Omit its anomalous
  3538-cc figure and unverified gearbox. Its discussion of the 1985 twin-plenum
  development does not establish the selected collection car's intake type.
  No extra single/twin-plenum entry or race tune is counted.
- **Swift:** Publicly indexed factory history identifies the 1986 three-door
  GTi's 1.3-litre twin-cam four-valve engine and 74 kW/101 PS. Direct retrieval
  was unavailable. Base-model mass, the GS engine, second-generation speed and
  later Swift Sport transmission data are excluded.
- **Kadett:** Read the German factory engineering paragraph, selecting its 1988
  catalysed 150-PS reference. Omit uncatalysed 156-PS figures and acceleration/
  speed not separated by catalyst state. Retrospectives differ on launch
  timing; no universal debut-date claim, DTM tune or Superboss specification
  is imported.
- **Calibra:** The Italian 2022 factory retrospective establishes 1992 Turbo
  4x4, 150 kW and six speeds. The 2019 factory account describes the integrated
  turbocharger/exhaust manifold. Omit the base model's 0.26 drag coefficient,
  ambiguously adjacent V6 performance wording, inferred displacement/ratios
  and ITC race-car hardware.
- **BX:** All three original brochure scans and enlarged text areas were read.
  Preserve native bhp/mph and 0–60-mph wording. Omit unprinted displacement,
  rating rpm and mass; do not mix later catalyst outputs, BX 4TC race hardware
  or the factory history's ordinary 1982 engine table into this reference.
