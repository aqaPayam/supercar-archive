after checking the entire 717-record catalogue, including Datsun/Nissan,

`data/icons-road-43.json`. No existing car is edited by this batch.

source in the public export. Unestablished details are omitted rather than
filled from secondary specification tables. Dedicated racers, prototypes and
extra same-generation derivatives remain outside this addition while the scope
questions are pending. First-generation S8 remains a research candidate; no
unreviewed S8 entry is imported.
