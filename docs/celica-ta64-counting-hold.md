# Celica TA64 GT-TS: researched, counting decision pending

Reviewed 2026-10-30 against the complete 921-record identity inventory.
No record, asset or qualifying count is added by this research.

## Why it is worth preserving

Toyota's [third-generation Celica history](https://www.toyota-global.com/company/history_of_toyota/75years/vehicle_lineage/car/id60010026A/index.html)
records 200 GT-TS homologation cars offered in October 1982. The [manufacturer's
TA64 account](https://media.toyota.co.uk/toyota-celica-twin-cam-turbo-ta64-group-b-rally-car/)
explicitly distinguishes 200 road cars from the evolutionary rally versions.
The latter's 2.1-litre engine, power and Hewland gearbox are competition
specifications, not road-car data. Both relevant pages were read.

The original [FIA B-239 form](https://historicdb.fia.com/sites/default/files/car_attachment/1601079001/homologation_form_number_239_group_b.pdf)
was downloaded and base printed pages 1–7 were individually inspected as
rendered images. It records 1 March 1983 homologation, 1,791 cc, inline four,
turbocharging, twin plugs, wet sump, rear drive and the base Toyota manual
gearset. Its separate additional gearbox column is not the base gearset.
Other rendered pages and the later extensions are not claimed as reviewed.
The file has 79 pages; no full-document audit is claimed. The 1.4 capacity
multiplier is a competition classification, not physical displacement.

## Why counting it needs the pending boundary

The inventory already has `TOY-SUPRA-A60-1981`, the MA61 Celica XX 2800 GT.
Toyota's [original 2 July 1981 launch](https://global.toyota/en/detail/7602652)
explicitly groups XX, liftback and coupe within one Celica series. The relevant
complete introduction, range description and engine discussion were read.
Its [Celica XX lineage account](https://www.toyota-global.com/company/history_of_toyota/75years/vehicle_lineage/car/id60010216/)
also describes Celica-derived construction and the later move to an independent
Supra platform. That page was read in full. The [Toyota UK Supra history](https://mag.toyota.co.uk/history-of-the-toyota-supra/)
was read through its relevant A60 discussion: common architecture and rear
body, but extended wheelbase/front end and six-cylinder powertrain.

The GT-TS is not a spelling alias of the MA61. It has a different body and
powertrain with a meaningful homologation purpose. However, the original
manufacturer's series grouping makes separate counting dependent on the
still-pending preference for substantially different factory derivatives.
Do not silently treat the related XX as absent, claim unrelated platforms,
or increase the qualifying count while that boundary remains undecided.

## Photo research retained

[TTTNIS's GT-TS photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:1982_Toyota_Celica_GT-TS.jpg)
and its CC0 metadata were read; the 1280-pixel source rendition was downloaded
and individually inspected. It shows a white road coupe with rise-up lamps
and fender mirrors. The already obscured plate/face and visible wheels are
not evidence of exact engine tune or originality. It was not copied into the
repository because no car is being imported.

This is a counting hold, not a rejection of the car's sporting significance.
Preserve the evidence and revisit when the existing variant question is answered.
