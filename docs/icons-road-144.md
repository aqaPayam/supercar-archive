## Admission and identity

The High Speed is a defining Swiss sporting-GT programme. Its case combines
contemporary technical development and driving coverage with enduring
recognition in a [later firsthand feature](https://www.classicandsportscar.com/features/monteverdi-375l-taking-greats).
Rarity, price, founder anecdotes and decorative differences alone are not
admission reasons. No exclusive fastest or first-Swiss-supercar claim is made.

A fresh complete 940-record identity inventory was checked for Monteverdi,
High Speed/Highspeed, 375 S/L and spacing aliases, and 375/4. No existing road
family was found. Production-road bodies, engine options, years and chassis
are grouped once conservatively, including further S/L/C/4 body references.
Hai prototypes, dedicated racers and replicas do not become additional records.

## Period technical reference

The [Swiss Transport Museum catalogue](https://sammlung.verkehrshaus.ch/de/collection/item/175500)
identifies a single-page original offprint, *Monteverdi - Weiterentwicklung
im Detail*, Automobil Revue 45/1973, p.45, inventory VA-65405. The
[original facsimile JPEG](https://sammlung.verkehrshaus.ch/multimedia/6/multimedia-143316.large.jpg)
was downloaded and individually viewed. Enlarged reading panels were used
for the model-1974, engine/cooling, comfort and short-drive sections. The
byline is retained only as **R. G.**; no expanded author name is assumed.
An initial generic download received HTTP 403; an ordinary browser-agent
request with the catalogue page as referrer retrieved the public facsimile.

All eleven attributes map to that original report. It discusses both the
375 L 2+2 and 375/4, so shared updates and the explicitly described coupe
transmission are used without borrowing saloon dimensions or seating.
The selected year is the article's model-1974 reference, not a family start
or a claimed registration date for a photographed chassis.

The standard engine's output is explicitly the reported German-approved
DIN value after emissions changes. Earlier gross figures are not substituted.
The optional 7-litre Hemi and the separate Hai drive are not the selected
engine/car. Rounded displacement is retained. The enlarged oil sump is
not total engine-oil capacity; the expansion vessel is not total coolant
capacity. The brief coupe/saloon drives do not supply timed performance.
No exact displacement conversion, dimensions, mass, torque conversion,
maximum speed, acceleration, tyre-size interpretation or price is imported.

The [1971 375/4 factory-sheet catalogue entry](https://sammlung.verkehrshaus.ch/de/collection/item/175480)
