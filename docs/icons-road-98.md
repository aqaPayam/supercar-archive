## S6 plus saloon — October 1996 German factory technical reference

The C4 S6 plus was quattro GmbH’s first complete high-performance model as a vehicle manufacturer, preceding its later RS programmes. Its reworked V8, six-speed manual and permanent quattro make it a substantive milestone in Audi Sport’s road-car engineering history.

C4 S6 plus saloon, German offering as of October 1996, original Audi factory technical sheet preserved by the Internet Archive. Select the left sedan column: 5.6-second 0–100 km/h and 1695 kg unladen weight, not the Avant’s 5.7 seconds/1745 kg. The common engine table gives 4172 cc V8, four overhead camshafts/four valves per cylinder, 11.6:1, 240 kW at 6500 rpm and 400 Nm at 3500 rpm under 80/1269/EEC. Native kW/Nm are retained; 326 PS is not mechanical bhp. Six-speed manual, Torsen centre differential and dual-mass flywheel are explicit; a secondary single-mass claim is not adopted. Performance and governed 250 km/h are factory claims.
