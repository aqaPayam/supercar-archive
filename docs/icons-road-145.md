## Admission and complete identity review

The Italia represents the transatlantic sporting hybrid: bespoke Italian
steel bodywork and a tube-frame chassis with Ford V8 power and four-wheel
disc brakes. The [original firsthand drive](https://rememberroad.com/intermeccanica-italia-italianhis-american-with-an-identity-crisis/)
and [manufacturer history](https://intermeccanica.com/history/)
support this sporting identity. Rarity and appearance alone are not admission
reasons; no exclusive first or measured-performance claim is necessary.

A fresh complete 941-record identity inventory was checked for Intermeccanica,
Griffith, Omega, Torino, Italia and Apollo aliases. Eight unrelated matches
were reviewed: Ferrari 458, Bugatti EB110 text, Lotus Carlton/Omega,
Maserati Biturbo text, modern TVR Griffith, Triumph GT6 text, Gumpert Apollo
and Ford Torino. None identifies this original Intermeccanica road family.

The manufacturer's account links the Griffith project, Omega stage and
Torino later renamed Italia. These stages, bodies, engine versions and
individual chassis are grouped conservatively once. This does not assert
mechanical identity across every stage. The earlier Apollo project and
later Indra are separate research subjects, not extra admissions here.
Dedicated racers, prototypes and Porsche replicas are not admitted.
