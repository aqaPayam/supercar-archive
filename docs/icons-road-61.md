## Evidence boundaries

Mercedes-Benz's factory SLC retrospective supplies the selected introduction,
road-car power and body features. Family-level seating, wheelbase and pre-1980
automatic are kept distinct from competition hardware. The later 500 SLC,
four-speed update and race outputs are excluded. FIA's model record corroborates
homologation identity; its gallery images were viewed, not the full form.
Precise displacement and homologation-day claims are deliberately omitted.

Bill Boddy's actual June 1970 Scimitar driving account supplies the selected
automatic, Ford V6 and chassis/body details. It balances enthusiastic handling
assessment with steering, space and quality faults. The 2014 archive date is
not the test year. British Motor Museum corroborates the original GTE concept;
its later SE5A figures are excluded. OCR-sensitive tyre dimensions and measured
performance are not imported.

The Hellenic Motor Museum's own Rivolta record supplies its 1966 reference and
native engine figures; the bhp rating standard remains unspecified. Iso's own
heritage supplies platform construction and Bertone four-seat packaging, while
describing multiple engine/transmission options. No gearbox is inferred for the
museum car. No Grifo specifications, converted power, current value or uncertain
production total is transferred.

Reliant brochure scans on one public host failed certificate verification;
verification was not disabled. The club brochure catalogue identifies scans as
member access, so those scans were not used. Public contemporary driving and
