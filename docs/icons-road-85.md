# Renault 12 Gordini — batch 85

Reviewed 30 October 2026. One original Renault 12 sporting-road family is
admitted through its R1173 Gordini, without counting ordinary 12s, model years,
badges, later equipment or competition evolutions separately.

## Admission and identity

The R1173 Renault 12 Gordini carried the Gordini sporting-saloon tradition from the rear-engined R8 into a front-wheel-drive programme. Its dedicated 1565 cc engine, ventilated front discs and stripped Group 1 homologation specification made it a real performance derivative, with a lasting place in the Gordini Cup and Renault’s driver-development history. One original Renault 12 sporting family is counted; ordinary 12s, badges, model years, later equipment packages and prepared racing evolutions do not create additional entries.

The complete 868-car inventory was inspected. Searches covered Renault 12,
R12, R1173, 1173, Gordini, Dacia and, as an adjacent research lead, Renault 17,
R17 and R1317. No Renault 12/Gordini, R1173 or Dacia identity was present.
R12/R17 substring hits were Mercedes R129/R170/R171/R172 chassis labels,
not Renault aliases. The preserved R8 Gordini has a different rear-engine,
rear-drive programme; Renault expressly describes the R12's front-drive change.
Only the R12 sporting family is admitted here. No Renault 17, R15, ordinary
Dacia/Renault saloon, Gordini badge or prepared race car is added in this batch.

The road model belongs for its substantial Gordini homologation/Cup heritage
and change of performance-saloon architecture, not merely because a Renault 12
is old, uncommon or available in a museum. The linked factory history documents
the road-sporting purpose and differentiates the competition derivatives.

Renault's 38-page Gordini history was downloaded intact. Actual full pages at
PDF indexes 11, 12 and 13 (printed pages 12–14) were rendered and visually read:
the English R12 road-model account, its French counterpart and the subsequent
competition-history page. These distinguish the four-cylinder type 807-20,
1565 cc, 77 x 84 mm road engine rated at 113 ch DIN / 125 ch SAE at 6250 rpm,
front-wheel drive and ventilated front disc brakes. Both original power ratings
are retained separately rather than confused with each other or converted to
an unverified modern rating. The source explicitly describes optional bumpers
on the early stripped reference and subsequent equipment changes.

invented to fill unavailable fields. Other PDF pages were not all read. R17
pages were rendered as a research lead but not visually read or used to admit

fact or current record claim is imported. Its road top-speed, dimensions and
production count are also not imported or used to identify this particular car.
