## Admission and duplicate review

The Bearcat belongs for its established place in early American sports-car
history, minimal road bodywork and rear-transaxle engineering. The
[Stahls collection record](https://www.stahlsauto.com/automobiles/1914-stutz-series-4e-bearcat/)
and [Henry Ford museum overview](https://www.thehenryford.org/collections/explore/artifact/67472)
support its sporting significance. Royalty, rarity, auction price and raw
power are not the admission test. No exclusive first-American-sports-car
claim is necessary or imported.

A fresh complete 937-record identity inventory was checked for Stutz,
Bearcat/Bear Cat and Series E/4E/4-E aliases. There is no existing family.
Unrelated Testarossa, Galant, BMW Z4 E89 and Audi Typ 4E substring hits were
reviewed and do not identify Stutz models. Original road years, engines,
bodywork and individual chassis count once. Dedicated competition cars,
replicas, later revivals and body conversions are not additional records.
