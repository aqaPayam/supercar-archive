# Road-icon batch 128: original Audi S3

Reviewed 2026-10-30 against all 924 existing identities. One absent first-generation
8L sporting-road family brings the working inventory to 925: 530 curated road

This is not a certified qualifying count toward the 1,000-icon goal.

## Admission and identity

The original S3 helped establish the turbocharged, AWD premium hot-hatch
formula. Its historical influence and lasting appeal are supported by original
retrospective driving analysis, with a contemporary 1999 drive supporting its
sporting-road character. Reserved handling and numb steering in the later
review are retained; no universal handling-benchmark claim is made.

The full inventory and every Audi A3/S3/RS3, 8L/8P/8V/8Y identity were checked.
Only later-generation 8P, 8V and 8Y RS3 families are present. No original 8L
sporting-road family was stored. Count all 8L outputs, facelifts, years, markets
and equipment once, including the later 225 PS update.

## Selected reference and factory evidence

The selected reference is **spring 1999 Germany S3, six-speed manual**.
Sixteen of its 18 attributes use the [preserved Audi launch narrative](https://www.autointell.net/european_companies/volkswagen/audi-ag/audi-cars/audi-s3/audiag1124.htm).
Its complete relevant body was read through the German availability paragraph.
The text matches the separately indexed February 1999 AudiWorld launch release,
but full AudiWorld pages returned 403 and are not claimed as read. The linked
technical sheet leads to an A6 path and is not used as an S3 authority.
Factory 0-100 km/h is labelled as a claim, not an independently measured result.

Audi's [2026 generation history](https://www.audi.com/de/pressemitteilungen/30-jahre-audi-a3-drei-jahrzehnte-vorsprung-im-premium-kompaktsegment-18368)
was read in its introduction, complete 8L section, later-generation distinctions
and chronology. It controls inline-four configuration and two intercoolers,
and corroborates the original 154 kW/210 PS manual launch. The selected output
attribute uses 154 kW at 5800 rpm from the period narrative; its ambiguous
210 bhp label is not reused. Later 165 kW/225 PS is not a new record.
The ordinary five-door A3 is not assigned to this three-door S3.

No exact engine cc/code, mass, maximum speed, brake dimensions or rear-disc
ventilation is imported. Brake accounts differ by reference/update and do not
need to be resolved to import the bounded fields selected here. Quattro uses
a hydraulic multi-plate clutch; no fixed longitudinal centre-differential
system or torque split is inferred.

## Original road reporting and limits

The complete [BIL April 1999 launch drive](https://www.bilnorge.no/audi-provekjort-provekjort/audi-s3/280902)
body and panel were read. It describes gearbox operation and the balance
between sporting chassis and comfort on rough roads. German April availability
and Norwegian June arrival are distinguished. Numerical panel data, including
an apparent wheelbase-unit error, supply no imported attributes.

The complete [PistonHeads retrospective feature](https://www.pistonheads.com/features/ph-features/audi-s3--ph-origin-story/45979)
and both specification panels were read. Its historical argument supports the
premium hot-hatch significance, with limitations retained. The driven heritage
car is an MTM demonstrator kept in modified tune from a later 225 PS base.
The 250hp, torque, acceleration and BAM code are not original 1999 specifications,
despite the panel heading. Embedded magazine quotations are secondary accounts,
not independently inspected original tests. No numerical attribute maps here.

Exact source/attribute/fact mappings are in the [manifest](../data/icons-road-128.json).
