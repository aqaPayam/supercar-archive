## Admission and identity

The 1957 fuel-injected Bel Air joins a defining American performance and cultural icon with an early production fuel-injection programme. Its optional competition-cam 283 V8 advertised one gross horsepower per nominal cubic inch. GM Heritage documents the Bel Air family’s lasting collectible and generational significance.

Full identities were checked for Bel Air, Belair, Tri-Five, Trifive, Black Widow
and Ramjet aliases. All Chevrolet records were separately reviewed for 150/210
identities: existing families were Camaro, Chevelle, Chevy II / Nova, Cobalt,
Corvette and Impala. No target family was present. Years, bodies, trim levels
and alternate engines do not multiply the count. This record is a road Sport
Coupe, not a Corvette or a Black Widow racing special.

## Technical evidence

The [original factory passenger-car sheets](https://chevy.oldcarmanualproject.com/chevyresto/57index.htm)
were visually read at full image size: 17 identifies Sport Coupe model 2454;
44 separates engine ratings; 45 distinguishes transmission combinations;
55 specifies injection and valve gear; 60 documents the manual gearbox.
The selected competition-cam injection engine advertises 283 gross bhp at
6200 rpm and 290 gross lb-ft at 4400 rpm. The regular-cam injected engine
is a different 250-hp option. Gross ratings are retained in native units.
No modern net conversion or measured road performance is inferred.

The close-ratio three-speed is selected from the factory table. No Corvette
four-speed, floor shift, base-engine compression ratio, installed axle ratio,
curb weight or production total is assigned. Sheet 56 was also viewed but its
graph describes a different engine rating and supplies no imported figures.
The Engineering Features PDF was downloaded but its scanned pages were not
visually audited; it is not imported evidence.

The [GM Heritage collection](https://www.gm.com/heritage/collection/chevrolet/1957-chevrolet-bel-air-convertible)
supports enduring historical significance. Its displayed convertible has a
245-hp dual-carb engine: none of that exhibit’s engine, weight, price or body
production figures are copied to the selected fuel-injected hardtop.

Sixteen attributes, exact evidence limits and mappings are retained in the
[manifest](../data/icons-road-106.json).
