## Technical and historical evidence boundaries

The Lupo factory history dates the six-speed update to 2001, separate from the
2000 five-speed introduction. Its own engine and vehicle profiles establish
the selected GTI row and layout. Ordinary 3L/FSI/diesel fields, type-code
ambiguities and mass claims are not transferred. evo's retrospective documents
the cult following and original-Golf comparison. Shane O'Donoghue's actual
2004 drive supports the engaging-engine/chassis case while acknowledging
understeer and electronic intervention. Conflicting or unqualified mass/test
figures remain excluded. A web-reader character-decoding failure did not prevent
reading the same public road report directly; no restricted page was accessed.

The initial Polo GT G40 uses its own 85-kW/148-Nm row, not the later
83-kW/150-Nm catalyst tune. The factory vehicle profile supports its layout.
Varying coupe-height ranges and unlisted transmission are not guessed. Cup and
modified record-car engines do not become stock road values. The speedometer's
240-km/h scale is not a maximum-speed specification. The concrete admission
case is the production small-car G-Lader programme, not its limited quantity.

Audi's own 2015 archival description explicitly identifies the second-generation
RS 3 Sportback and its original engine. The technology portal independently
confirms the selected power and torque. The rounded displacement class stays
rounded; adjacent RS Q3 and later TT RS fields are excluded. No 2017 facelift,
sedan or later engine technology is transferred. Audi's editorial-use photograph
is a factual source only; the archive uses a separate Commons photograph.

The 2021 factory account identifies the third-generation Sportback, its first-Audi
torque splitter and separately controlled rear clutches. The engine appendix
supplies its own displacement, seven-speed dual-clutch and quattro specification.
Optional adaptive dampers, semi-slicks, ceramics, increased speed limits and
record configurations are not universal equipment. The ambiguous day/month
display on the migrated site is not asserted as an exact launch date.
