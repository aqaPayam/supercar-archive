# Baseline eligibility audit checkpoint

Snapshot: 2026-10-29, 770 working records. This is an inventory checkpoint,
not a completed eligibility or semantic duplicate audit.

The 50 reviewed road-icon manifests contain 375 distinct identifiers. Subtracting
those identifiers from the actual database leaves the preserved 395-record
baseline. Its records have not automatically been approved under the new policy.
The raw 770 count must not be presented as 770 proven qualifying icons.

An exact case-folded, whitespace-trimmed comparison of manufacturer, model,
generation and variant found no identical baseline identity-text tuples. That
check cannot establish semantic uniqueness: aliases, body styles, optional
packages and differently described versions still require per-car review.
Same-generation variant counting remains a pending user boundary.

identity, specification or count, and does not certify the F50's remaining
technical, pricing or historical fields. The full baseline audit remains open.

A [second credit repair](baseline-media-credits-02.md) resolves 27 further
creator/licence placeholders across ten baseline profiles, after direct Commons

unchanged. This finishes the observed placeholder list, not a comprehensive
licensing or representative-image audit. A subsequent
[photograph scope repair](baseline-image-scope-01.md) qualifies the flagged

## Identity spot-check: 2022 V12 Vantage

Reviewed 2026-10-29 against the stored profiles and both original manufacturer
announcements. `AST-V12V-2022` is the coupe; `AST-V12V-ROAD-2022` is the Roadster.
They are different body derivatives of the same contemporary V12 Vantage
programme, not two aliases for the same body/reference and not independently
established vehicle generations merely because their stored generation labels
differ. The [coupe announcement](https://www.astonmartin.com/en/our-world/news/2022/3/16/introducing-the-new-v12-vantage-a-spectacular-finale-for-an-iconic-bloodline)
identifies a 333-car allocation; the [Roadster announcement](https://www.astonmartin.com/en-us/our-world/news/2022/8/20/introducing-the-new-v12-vantage-roadster-the-ultimate-expression-of-style-sound-and-speed)
identifies 249 open cars and explicitly describes shared suspension hardware
with a different damper tune. These are announced allocations, not survivor counts.

Both existing records remain preserved. Whether they contribute one or two
qualifying identities depends on the pending body/variant-counting decision.
This two-record identity check is not approval of the remaining baseline or a
fresh audit of every technical field, image, price or colour record.

## Identity spot-check: 2021 Porsche 992 GT3 Touring

Reviewed 2026-10-29 against both stored profiles and the original factory
announcements. `POR-911-GT3-9921-PDK` and `POR-911-GT3-9921-TOUR` belong to
the same contemporary 992 GT3 programme. Their different stored generation
labels do not establish two vehicle generations.

The [February 2021 launch](https://newsroom.porsche.com/it/ppdb/2021/02/nuova-porsche-911-gt3-un-concentrato-di-sportivit.html)
describes the winged road GT3, shared 4.0-litre engine and manual/PDK choices.
The [June 2021 Touring announcement](https://newsroom.porsche.com/en/2021/products/porsche-911-gt3-touring-package-premiere-24794.html)
explicitly describes Touring as an equipment package for that GT3. It replaces
the fixed wing with an extending spoiler, changes exterior/interior detailing,
and is available with manual or PDK. It is not a new generation, while its
aerodynamic equipment differs from the winged reference.

Both existing records are preserved. Their qualifying count remains pending
the significant-variant boundary. A gearbox choice or distinct generation text
alone does not justify an additional identity. This source-backed identity
check does not certify every technical field or photograph in either profile.

## Identity spot-check: S13 Silvia Q’s and K’s

Reviewed 2026-10-29 against the stored source references and both Nissan
heritage profiles. `NIS-SIL-S13-QS-JP` and `NIS-SIL-S13-KS-JP` represent two
engine grades within the same fifth-generation S13 programme. Nissan's
[1988 Q’s reference](https://www.nissan-global.com/EN/HERITAGE_COLLECTION/silvia_qs.html)
identifies the naturally aspirated CA18DE, while the
[1989 K’s reference](https://www.nissan-global.com/EN/HERITAGE_COLLECTION/silvia_ks.html)
identifies the turbocharged CA18DET. Both pages explain the common 1988 S13
introduction and distinguish the later SR20 engine change.

The different heritage-reference years do not create separate generations.
The engine/induction difference is substantive, so these are not identical
technical references either. Both records remain preserved; whether they
contribute one or two qualifying identities awaits the significant-variant
boundary. This checks identity and source context, not every specification,
photograph or provenance claim in either stored profile.

## Outstanding decisions and evidence

A read-only media inventory screen at the 748-record checkpoint finds 1,002
stored media rows belonging to the preserved baseline. All have nonempty
creator and licence fields; none matches the screened placeholder phrases
“various,” “unknown,” “see source,” “Commons contributors,” or “not specified.”
This is a field-content screen only. It does not verify each attribution,
licence version, photograph identity or technical implication against the
original source, and does not complete the required media audit.

The existing Hyundai Verna Iran 1.5 manual is already flagged outside the
confirmed performance-icon scope in [the admission policy](curation1000.md).
It remains preserved rather than becoming precedent for ordinary additions.
A final qualifying total requires a record-by-record significance and identity
review of the baseline, resolution of the variant/racing boundaries and checks
of technical provenance, road/competition separation and representative image
licensing. Neither the absence of identical strings nor passing import tests
proves these historical and editorial requirements.
