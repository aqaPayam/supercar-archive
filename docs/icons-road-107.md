## Admission and identity

The original Fury is an important early American performance-road programme, pairing a dual-carb V8 with a deliberately uprated torsion-bar chassis. Racer Brown’s contemporary 1957 first-hand test establishes exceptional period handling significance while documenting braking limitations, supporting admission beyond styling, rarity or later film fame.

The complete inventory was checked for Fury, Belvedere, Savoy and Christine
aliases. Existing Plymouth families are Road Runner Superbird, Barracuda and
Valiant / Duster; no target was present. Count the original 1956–1958 Fury
performance-road programme once across years, bodies, engines and associated
Belvedere identities. Later full-size namesakes, Super Stock race cars and
film replicas do not supply this road record’s specifications.

## Technical evidence

The [dedicated 1957 factory showroom folder](https://over-drive-magazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/1957-Plymouth-Fury-Brochure-1-4.pdf)
is a four-page scan of 5,391,010 bytes. Full PDF indexes 1 and 3 were visually
read, including an enlarged engineering region on 3. Other pages were not
individually audited. Twenty-one attributes retain this folder’s engine,
manual-transmission, chassis, tyre and dimension specification in native units.

The folder lists 290 hp at 5400 rpm and 325 lb-ft at 4000 rpm, dual-carb
V-800 equipment, standard three-speed synchromesh and heavy-duty suspension.
Its quoted 206.0/79.4/53.5-inch dimensions differ from some road-test figures;
the labels explicitly identify the factory folder. It lists blackwall tyres
as standard and whitewalls as optional. Its manual axle listing differs from
some compilations, so an exact installed axle ratio is not assigned. Estimated
shipping weight is not imported as curb mass. No modern net conversion.

The [May 1957 HOT ROD first-hand test](https://www.hotrod.com/features/plymouth-fury-vs-stock-may-1957-982-374-16-1)
by Racer Brown supports admission through period handling evaluation and
records brake fade. Its test-car setup and subsequent tuning experiments are
kept separate from this factory-reference record. No acceleration, revised
ignition, linked carburettors, suggested modifications or race figures are
imported as factory performance. The Over-Drive fact-sheet page was used to
locate original documents; its secondary summaries are not technical evidence.

and specification limits.
