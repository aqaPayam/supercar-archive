## Admission and identity

The DB11 introduced a new bonded-aluminium architecture and twin-turbo V12 at a turning point in Aston Martin’s road-car programme. A contemporary production-car test supports its engine and tactile grand-touring character, with noise and less overtly sporting behaviour acknowledged. One DB11 core family is represented across V12, V8, AMR and Volante versions.

The full inventory has no DB11/DB 11/AE31 identity. DB9 is the previous
architecture. The existing DBS Superleggera required a deeper relationship
check: the [original DBS launch](https://www.astonmartin.com/en-us/our-world/news/2018/6/26/introducing-the-new-dbs-superleggera-two-illustrious-names-one-magnificent-super-gt)
explicitly describes evolution of DB11 architecture and aerodynamics, while
identifying DBS as the Vanquish S replacement. These shared components are
acknowledged rather than ignored or treated as proof of an alias.

The [manufacturer’s core-range presentation](https://www.astonmartin.com/-/media/corporate/corporatearchive/documents/results-centre/2018---results-centre/q4---2018/aston-martin-lagonda-preliminary-results-2018---presentation.pdf?rev=d161929f3c2b4a77bc634b6ce79d0c91)
separately lists DB11, Vantage and DBS Superleggera on printed slide 22 (PDF
index 21). DB11’s own variants are V12, V12 AMR, V8 and Volante. The relevant
web PDF text was read; a screenshot request returned no inspectable image and
is not claimed as visual review. No financial figures or complete 43-page audit
is claimed. This supports one DB11 core identity and no extra engine, body,
AMR or year entries. Existing baseline derivatives still await the broader audit.

## Technical evidence and limits

is dated 28 February 2017. Its relevant full body was read, including structure,
V12 output, aerodynamic ducts, modes and technology. All 17 selected attributes
map here. The reference is the original 608-PS/600-BHP V12, not a later AMR,
V8 or DBS tune. Displacement remains nominal 5.2 litres; exact cc, peak speeds,
torque plateau, injection type, mass, dimensions, tyres and brake sizes are not
invented or borrowed from another market. Factory speed/acceleration stay claims.

The [2017 production-car test](https://www.caranddriver.com/reviews/a15088424/2017-aston-martin-db11-test-review/)
was read in its relevant complete body and panel. It supports the engine,
new architecture and tactile GT character, with noise and sporting limitations
acknowledged. No US measured figure or technical field is imported. The earlier
Motor Trend first drive was also read and explicitly says its launch cars were
pre-production; it is research context, not proof that final production software
retained its faults. No pre-production test figures supply attributes.

The English brochure exceeded web-fetch size and returned 403 via ordinary
HTTP; the French brochure also exceeded web-fetch size. Neither was downloaded
or visually read, so their search excerpts are not controlling evidence.
Exact mappings remain in the [manifest](../data/icons-road-121.json).
