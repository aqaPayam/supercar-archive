## Evidence boundaries

Octane's actual driving feature was originally published in issue 210,
December 2020. It concerns road-registered 1937 BYG 7, chassis 12235/R.
The selected fields retain that reference's 4453 cc Meadows pushrod six,
twin SU HV5s, G10 manual, rear drive, beam/live axles, leaf springs, dampers,
rod-operated drums and steering. It supplies first-hand praise for response
and touring, with clear steering and poor-road criticisms. Its implausible
885 kg mass is not repeated. Claimed power, performance figures and an inferred
factory standard are not imported. Accessibility modifications made for the
first owner are individual adaptations, not standard Rapide equipment.

Bonhams' original February 2022 S91 catalogue distinguishes an original 1931
road-registered S-Type from modern continuations. Only explicitly described
standard-programme fields are used: Meadows six, 4.5-litre class, twin 40 mm
SUs, dual ignition, underslung rear frame and finned drums. These describe
original programme architecture and do not certify every current part of
S91. The marque club's public history separately corroborates the low chassis
and wheelbase. Its members-only archive was not accessed.

No exact disputed Invicta displacement, power/peak-speed combination, universal
rear-axle ratio or production total is silently reconciled from later histories.
The 158 bhp racing tune, stripped racing-car performance and 100 mph slogan
are not standard road specifications. The catalogue's separate 1936-labelled
S314B continuation was rejected as the reference for this original family.

The Delage D8-120 remains a research candidate. An original auction catalogue
and museum exhibition give useful technical/design evidence, but its admission
under the performance-icon policy needs a clearer performance-specific case.
No Delage car or photo is added to this batch merely because the data exist.
