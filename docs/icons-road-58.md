## Technical evidence boundaries

The ATS-V uses General Motors bulletin PI1515, July 2015, pages 1–3, hosted by
NHTSA. Its pages were extracted and visually read: 2016 coupe/sedan, LF4 twin-
turbo V6, direct injection, variable valve timing, final 464 hp / 346 kW,
603 Nm and six-speed Tremec manual with rev matching/no-lift shifting. The
selected body is the sedan. A contemporary first-hand manual-sedan test supports
handling and shift quality, while criticising the engine sound; no measured
performance or test-car options are transferred. The final GM bulletin resolves
the order guide's inconsistent 343.3-kW conversion. The preliminary 455-hp press
release is not the final rating. No additional coupe or automatic is counted.

The CT4-V Blackwing uses its original one-page 2022 Cadillac factsheet, downloaded
with verified TLS and visually read. Its 3.6-litre twin-turbo V6, native 472 US hp /
445 lb-ft, standard six-speed manual, Magnetic Ride Control 4.0, rear electronic
limited-slip differential and Brembo brakes are not the larger CT5-V Blackwing's
V8 or the CT6-V's 4.2-litre engine. A first-hand 2022 10Best award evaluation
supports its performance-saloon admission while acknowledging interior limits.
Optional automatic, aero and recording equipment, estimated acceleration and
inconsistent quarter-mile text are excluded. An older mixed-field brochure HTML
and a timed-out guide are not needed for this evidence.

The fourth Megane uses Renault Suisse's September 2019 Italian brochure,
particularly pages 11, 15 and 20, read as text and rendered pages. Its 1798-cc
16-valve turbo four, 221 kW, 400 Nm, manual six-speed, two seats, Torsen
limited-slip differential and lighter H-type rear axle belong to Trophy-R.
Page 23's ordinary R.S./Trophy descriptions do not establish Trophy-R 4CONTROL.
Carbon wheels and 390-mm carbon-ceramic brakes are optional. The ambiguous
Italian hood-material translation is omitted rather than silently corrected.
Weight-saving and lap-record statements are not imported as universal figures.
This is one absent fourth-generation performance family, not a second grade of
the archived third-generation 275 Trophy-R.

The third CTS-V uses the original 2016 US one-page factory factsheet, downloaded
with verified TLS and visually read. Its supercharged 6.2-litre V8, SAE-certified
640 hp, 630 lb-ft, rear-wheel drive, eight-speed 8L90 automatic, Gen 3 magnetic
damping and standard carbon hood are selected. The 200-mph maximum is explicitly
a manufacturer claim. The carbon vent, splitter, spoiler and diffuser are
available equipment, not universal. No manual, earlier LS6/LSA engine or later
CT5-V equipment is mixed in. An independently downloaded GM PI1529 bulletin also
identifies the third-generation reference, but is not needed for imported fields.

The R56 MINI uses the 2012 global factory press kit. The reference year denotes
that announcement/presentation/production account, not an inferred US model year.
Its 1.6-litre turbo four produces 160 kW and 260 Nm, with 280 Nm only temporarily
under overboost. It retains a six-speed manual and adjustable coilovers with
six-piston front brakes. Brake-based EDLC is not a mechanical limited-slip
differential. Alternative tyres, lap times and additional R53 GP counts are
excluded. The F56 selects the original German November 2019 release for March
2020 deliveries: 2.0-litre turbo four, 225 kW/450 Nm, eight-speed automatic and
mechanical front differential lock. It does not inherit the R56 manual or its
brake-based control. A historical description of earlier GP engines in that
release is not used. Concepts, GP Pack, cosmetics and later model years are not
new generations. Both MINI sources were read as the original public factory
articles through the web reader; redirected modern-newsroom HTML is not evidence.
