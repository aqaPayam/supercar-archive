# Road-icon batch 103: original Monaro GTS 327

Reviewed 2026-10-30 against all 899 existing identity rows. One absent original
Monaro road family brings the working inventory to 900 records: 505 curated
road additions across 103 manifests plus the preserved 395-record baseline.
The 1,000-qualifying-icon goal and baseline audit remain open; raw inventory
is not a certified qualifying count.

## Admission and duplicate review

The original Monaro GTS 327 belongs as an Australian performance-coupe icon: its V8, close-ratio manual, limited-slip axle and supporting chassis equipment formed a purposeful road package with Bathurst-winning heritage. The State Library’s Holden collection history records the original coupe introduction and racing connection.

The complete identifier/manufacturer/family/generation/variant inventory was
checked for Monaro, HK/HT/HG and GTS 327 identities. The only existing Monaro
is the distinct revived V2 CV8, introduced in 2001; existing Torana programmes
do not represent the original coupe. Count the original HK/HT/HG road family
once, using this 1968 Australian HK GTS 327 reference. No extra engine, axle,
year or cosmetic package count is added. Dedicated racers, replicas and later
HT/HG engine specifications are excluded from this road record.

## Primary evidence

The [original HK Monaro factory catalogue](https://xr793.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/1968-Holden-HK-Monaro-AUS.pdf)
is a 3,427,851-byte, seven-page scan. Full PDF indexes 3 and 5 (printed 6–7
and 10–11) were visually read, including enlarged text regions for the engine,
GTS package, equipment chart and general specifications. All-page thumbnails
were navigation only; other full pages were not individually audited.

Twenty source-mapped attributes retain factory advertised output and native
units. The two rear-axle ratios remain brochure alternatives, not an assumed
single specification. GTS 327 equipment is separated from the 186/307 engines,
base Monaro options and later models. Power steering remains optional. No
modern net/kW conversion, measured acceleration, maximum speed, race tuning
or inferred production total is supplied.

The [State Library's Holden collection finding aid](https://archival.collections.slsa.sa.gov.au/brg/BRG213_37_HoldenHK_serieslist.pdf)
was read as PDF text. Its first page records the July 1968 coupe introduction
and identifies GTS 327 as a Bathurst winner. It supports admission and identity,
not detailed race or road-engine values. Listed release files/catalogues were
not independently examined, and the two-millionth Holden is not a Monaro count.
The discovered historic Group Nc information sheet concerns racing approval;
it is not road technical authority and was not used for imported specifications.
