## Admission, reference and sources

### Ford Torino / Fairlane

The Talladega is a road-production icon of the NASCAR aero-war era. Its reshaped nose, grille and bumper were developed for aerodynamic performance rather than cosmetic differentiation. The factory road version paired that body programme with a 428 Cobra Jet and automatic transmission. One original Torino/Fairlane family entry captures this engineering and motorsport connection without assigning race-engine hardware to the street car.

### AMC Rambler American

The Hurst SC/Rambler turned AMC’s compact Rogue hardtop into a purpose-built road muscle car. A 390 V8, close-ratio four-speed, Twin-Grip axle, front discs and upgraded chassis equipment made the package substantially more than paint and stripes. Its established Hurst collaboration and drag-oriented road-car concept earn one original Rambler American family entry; colour schemes and dealer racing modifications do not add counts.
